# Item-selection evidence packet

As of **2026-11-01**. This is a small curated evidence pack for a proposed planner,
not a demographic study, implemented planner, or validated art-production system.
Source text was checked by the upstream research handoff; this packet does not
claim a new fetch, audience measurement, or image assessment.

## Authority and use

1. Local task, collection explanation, and art guide (`L_TASK`, `L_COLLECTION`,
   `L_ART`) govern this exercise: 3–5 variants, ten cards per set, three movie
   genres, all four non-gold staging categories per set; rarity changes no art.
2. Publisher storefront/help text supplies **game-specific product context**,
   not common rendering rules or measured players. Keep Gardenscapes, Homescapes,
   Township, and Fishdom separate; no target game was named in the assignment.
3. Naming research and specialist object history support only their actual
   populations/objects/claims. They do not establish recognition of proposed cards.
4. Trends, optional web search, and model suggestions are discovery/heuristics,
   not verified recognition, audience preference, or calibrated probability.
5. Editorial assumptions are explicitly marked in [playrix_profile.json](playrix_profile.json).
   Fix the set's premise/frame before assembling its roster. Warm/playful readable
   objects, evergreen-first selection, and non-graphic accessible genre treatments
   are proposed defaults, **not Playrix policy**.

[SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json) records scope and limits per source. Storefront content
ratings and supported languages do not identify player ages, countries, gender,
or country weights. Those fields and `target_game` remain unknown.

## Thematic text hints (2026-11-01 supplement)

Seven librarian-verified source records extend the pack to **21 records**, retaining
all original IDs. Verification here means the upstream librarian checked source
text, not that this packet writer fetched it or verified candidate form, audience
recognition, or image rights. The BFI publication date is 2014-01-29; museum object
dates are not publication dates. Other new publication dates remain unknown.

[object_reference_hints.json](object_reference_hints.json) provides six small
concept mappings: compass, binoculars, observatory collection context, pie-gag
context, wand and crystal ball. Match normalized **whole words/token sequences**,
not arbitrary substrings (`wand` must not match `wanderer`); filter by declared
genre and actual concept relevance, not just a broad theme. These are suggestions,
not mandatory objects or exhaustive synonym/identity rules.

`identity_text_suggestion` permits a textual identity-reference suggestion with
unverified assessment and unknown rights; it is not visually verified form.
`genre_context` stays context only: do not attach the observatory collection or
pie-gag articles as automatic object identity references. Museum photographs were
not downloaded or visually reviewed. The compass search snippet was wrong; its
record uses the actual page. Treat snippets as discovery, not trusted evidence.
Preserve all hint limitations and distinguish fictional function from real claims;
do not import violent routines, franchise imagery or decorative parts not supported
by the source. All reference/image rights remain unknown/unverified.
