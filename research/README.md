## Landscape check, 2026-11-07

What changed since the literature review, and what the project tried. Sources were read on 2026-11-07.

- **Open models now fit one consumer GPU.** Qwen-Image-2.1 (7B, 10 references, research licence) and FLUX 3 Image Klein (5.6B, 10 references, gated, free under $5M revenue) run through `diffusers` 0.42 with no remote code. Qwen-Image-2.1 was tried in `experiments/generators.py`; FLUX 3 Klein needs its licence accepted on Hugging Face first. ([Qwen-Image-2.1](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1), [FLUX 3 Klein](https://bfl.ai/blog/flux-3-image-klein))
- **Closed models:** the GPT image line behind Codex takes several references; Nano Banana Pro has an explicit style-reference role and was tried through the Antigravity CLI; Recraft V4 Styles and Midjourney V8 expose a style-strength control. ([OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation), [Google](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/image-generation), [Recraft](https://www.recraft.ai/docs/api-reference/models/recraft-v4-styles))
- **Studios train a style.** Scenario, Layer and Leonardo train a LoRA on a studio's own art, often one per asset type, and edit LoRAs on before/after pairs. The brief provides no LoRA, so training one on the 160 reference cards is the obvious next step: `diffusers` ships a LoRA script for FLUX 3 Klein, about 1-3 hours here. ([Scenario](https://help.scenario.com/en/articles/basics-of-model-training), [Layer](https://www.layer.ai/case-study/games-united), [script](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/examples/dreambooth/README_flux3_klein.md))
- **3D renders as structure.** Depth control holds contours too rigidly; edit models copy the render's finish, which is the gloss the producer saw on blockout cards. Practice is an edit model with a style LoRA, or a matte structure pass instead of the shaded render.
- **Measuring style.** Raw CSD cosine fails as an absolute score and is corrected for hubness by CSD+ (CSLS); here it moved agreement with the producer from 128 to 132 of 218 pairs, within noise (`experiments/csd_plus.py`). VLM judges do better pairwise than pointwise and show position bias, which is why the director judges both orders. ([CSD+](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09030), [GenArena](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06013))
- **What the tools lack that this project has:** none of the products measures against a human, runs blind twins or builds geometry for construction.

## Overnight check, 2026-11-08

Why ours read grainier to the outside rater, and what finds or fixes parts drawn from two cameras. Sources read 2026-11-07/08; scripts `experiments/smooth_check.py`, `perspective_check.py`, `blockout_drift.py`, results in the matching `runs/` folders.

**Smooth surfaces.**
- **No measured comparison exists for GPT-image models.** Prompt libraries agree, without A/B numbers, on naming the medium (a 3D render) and not naming tactile materials. [OpenAI's prompting guide](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/multimodal/image-gen-models-prompting-guide) says to ask explicitly for real texture when it is wanted. Our style text asks that "each material looks like itself, with a little quiet detail", and many briefs name a material (felt, leather, wood).
- **A pixel measure now shows the grain on twins.** The flat share is the share of surface pixels (inside the subject, away from strong edges) whose 3x3 lightness deviation is under 0.004. Against their own real cards, twins have the lower flat share in every round: final 32 of 40 (p 0.0001); old, r3 and r9 p 0.002-0.02. The gap fades at 0.01, so the grain is low-amplitude. It does not tell which twins the producer caught (p 0.47), and as a judge of the 218 pairs the producer decided it picks their side 104 times (CSD: 128), so it checks one complaint over a round; it does not judge a card. On tonight's arms, the smooth line `ns` cut the gap from -0.109 (control `n0`) to -0.032, nearer the real card on 22 of 40 twins (p 0.04). The camera line `nc` left it at -0.113.
- **Material nouns bring the grain.** The 12 twins whose brief names a material lose twice the flat share of the other 28 against the same real objects: -0.197 against -0.092 in `n0` (p 0.007), and alike in `final`, `r3`, `r9` and `nc` (p 0.008-0.05). Not in `old` (p 0.32), whose text asked for clean surfaces, nor with the smooth line (-0.046 against -0.018, p 0.47). On the pre-final cards, the 24 of 81 that name a material lean the same way (fine texture AUC 0.63, p 0.07).
- **Smoothing after drawing does not help by CSD.** A guided filter or a domain-transform filter on the full drawing before the downscale restores the flat share and keeps 94-99% of the part-edge strength, and by eye the leather grain is gone (`runs/smooth_check/sheets/`). But on the 40 final-style twins, CSD to the twin's own real card did not improve: the light filter came out closer on 18 of 40 (p 0.51), the strong filter and the domain transform on 13 of 40 (p 0.01, further). On the pre-final cards the filters also push fine texture below the real cards. Not adopted without a blind look. ([guided filter](https://people.csail.mit.edu/kaiming/publications/pami12guidedfilter.pdf), [domain transform](https://www.inf.ufrgs.br/~eslgastal/DomainTransform/))

**Parts from two cameras.**
- **The detectors in the literature work on scenes, not on single objects.** Classifiers that see only perspective fields or line segments separate generated scenes from real ones at AUC 0.72-0.97. They were trained on 525k real indoor and street photos and generated versions of them ([Sarkar et al., CVPR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.17138)). ControlVP fixes vanishing points by a user-guided inpaint, and only on buildings ([WACV 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07504)). GeoCalib fits one camera to a perspective field ([ECCV 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06704)). Nothing was found that finds or fixes camera mixing between the parts of one stylized object.
- **GeoCalib finds none of our breaks.** It ran on 18 cards the producer called off in perspective, the outside rater's 10 broken cards, 156 unflagged cards of ours and the 160 real cards. Its confidence on cards is near zero (median 0.001 of 1), and its estimated cameras of ours and of real cards do not differ. The best of nine scores, the 90th-percentile up residual, reached AUC 0.69 against good cards (p 0.01 before correction). At 10% false alarms on real cards it found 3 of 18 and 0 of 10.
- **Blockout cards break in the blockout or the setting, not by drifting from the model.** Drawings stay inside their render's outline (median IoU 0.90): 0.91 for broken cards against 0.93 for the rest, p 0.41. By my eye on the five (`runs/blockout_drift/broken.png`; a reading, not a rating), three renders already look broken: the chair's floor runs at another angle and its far edge looks like a tilted horizon, the gate's path recedes steeply while the gate and its posts face the camera flat, and the hourglass discs are drawn as the render has them. In two the drawing adds a surface in its own perspective: the magnifier's map under a lens the render shows nearly face-on, and the door's paving. So the fix belongs in the blockout scene and the restyle prompt: model the surface the object rests on, with its lines, and do not let the drawing invent a surface.
- **Not tried.** Depth, normal or line ControlNets from a 3D render, which Codex cannot take; the Image API's [markup-guided edits](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/image-generation) (2.5/2.6 models), which Codex does not expose; lifting a drawn object to 3D and re-rendering it, with Hunyuan3D 2.1 ([runs on a 7900 XTX](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2/issues/52)) or TRELLIS.2 ([with patches](https://github.com/toastmanAu/trellis-2-rocm-comfyui)). No source measures any of them on this failure.

**Style LoRA on this GPU.** Feasible, untested for this style. `diffusers` ships FLUX 3 Klein LoRA scripts (text-to-image, and image-to-image pairs; examples rank 32, lr 1e-4, 2,000 steps at 1024 px; no VRAM, time or AMD notes). SimpleTuner has a ROCm install and lists FLUX 3 Klein and Qwen-Image 2.1; ai-toolkit is NVIDIA-only. BFL's guide puts a style at 20-40 images (dilution above 40), 1,200-2,000 steps, 1-3 h on consumer NVIDIA cards: so about 40 object cards of one finish, not all 160. Risks: Qwen-Image-2.1 went NaN at 1024 px in inference here, so start at 512-768 px with SDPA; FLUX 3 Klein is gated behind a licence the producer accepts. A LoRA does not fix what lost the bake-off (small objects, broken hard rules). An estimated half day, judged on the 40 twins by CSD and flat share, then blind. ([script](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/examples/dreambooth/README_flux3_klein.md), [SimpleTuner](https://docs.simpletuner.io/INSTALL/), [ai-toolkit](https://github.com/ostris/ai-toolkit), BFL [overview](https://docs.bfl.ai/flux_2/flux2_klein_training) and [style example](https://docs.bfl.ai/flux_2/flux2_klein_training_example))

**Other ideas.**
- **An edit LoRA needs aligned pairs.** Twins differ from their real cards in pose and framing, so they would teach composition changes. Aligned pairs would come from Codex redrawing each real card in its own finish, about 160 draws, with the LoRA learning the way back. No source tests this.
- **Material nouns in briefs.** The planner could name color and shape and leave out felt, leather or grain: a twin round to try beside the smooth line.
