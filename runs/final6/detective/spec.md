## Notes

- Composed on 2026-11-08 from the outside rater's best variant (v2) after a review of all three pre-final variants and the producer's earlier grades (experiments/plan_review.py, runs/night/plan_review.json).
- Flashlight: outside 3. Source: runs/prefinal/detective v2_01.
- Flash Camera: its twins in v1 and v3 got 3. Source: runs/prefinal/detective v2_02.
- Magnifier, floating, replaces the Map Magnifier (outside 2, broken: a lens drawn front-on while lying flat). Source: runs/prefinal/detective v3_01.
- Fedora: the genre's hat, on a floor. Source: runs/prefinal/detective v2_04.
- Badge Wallet: the badge, lying flat in its wallet. Source: runs/prefinal/detective v2_05.
- Evidence Bag: outside 3. Source: runs/prefinal/detective v2_06.
- Steel Safe replaces the Tape Roll (outside 3), which without text is only tape; the safe got outside 4. Source: runs/prefinal/detective v1_05.
- Doorknob Print: the small object in its exact spot. Source: runs/prefinal/detective v2_08.
- Squad Car: outside 4; carries the set's one emblem. Source: runs/prefinal/detective v2_09.
- Bloodhound: outside 3 in v1; the set's one animal. Source: runs/prefinal/detective v2_10.
- Colors rewritten under the rich-color rule adopted overnight (on the twins, closer to their real cards by CSD on 29 of 40): Flashlight: red body, silver head, yellow switch and light → rich red body, silver head ring, yellow switch and light; Flash Camera: black body, silver lens, small red button → red-brown body, silver lens ring, small yellow button, color words in the subject to match; Magnifier: silver rim, clear glass, red handle → silver rim, clear glass with white glint, glossy red handle; Fedora: tan felt, dark brown band → rich tan crown, deeper amber-tan brim, forest green band, color words in the subject to match; Steel Safe: dark grey steel, gold dial and handle → deep navy steel, gold dial and handle, silver hinges; Doorknob Print: brass knob, dark green door, grey powder print → brass knob, forest green door, grey powder print, color words in the subject to match; Bloodhound: tan and black fur, red collar → tan fur, red-brown ears and back, forest green collar, sky blue, light cyan → sky blue, light blue, color words in the subject to match.
- Kept by hand: the Squad Car stays black and white on a day sky, the colors that make it a police car (outside 4). The Doorknob Print keeps its warm yellow backdrop, so the set has one pink floor and one pink surface rather than three pinks.
- After the final draw, 2026-11-08 (runs/final3/detective): the Badge Wallet on a dark wooden table measured lightness 0.34, darker than any card in the real sets (lowest 0.44; real flat surfaces such as Tennis and Fruit Plate sit at 0.66-0.71), so it moves to a light honey-colored desk. The Evidence Bag's chosen drawing is an edit that had already moved it onto light wood and beat its dark draft.
- Flash Camera moves from sky blue to royal blue: with the Badge Wallet on light wood, the drawn set had five backdrop hues against six or seven on every real set, four of them in one cyan band, and the camera sat beside the cyan flashlight. Its blockout keeps its geometry; only the render backdrop changed.
- Fix round, 2026-11-08, after the producer's grades on experiments/final_review.py (Detective 4 of 5, mean 3.1).
- Flash Camera (3: "Should be more centered at the point of visual attention"; set: "off-center of the circular back, looks strange. Use other back"): diamonds instead of rings, and the blockout centers on the camera's body (k.focus) with a smaller flash dish.
- Magnifier (3: "Perspective looks a bit off"): the lens was turned 46 degrees from the camera while the handle ran across the card; the blockout now turns it to face the viewer.
- Fedora (3: "Is it floating? The shadow seems kinda strange"): drawn on a floor, it hovered above its own shadow. Now it floats on purple waves, as the Cowboy Hat and Top Hat that got 5.
- Badge Wallet to Police Badge (2: "Not a very recognizable badge"): a large shield with an eagle on top, a blue enamel center with a star and a blank banner, in a black wallet with an ID card; a new Blender model.
- Evidence Bag (3: "The ring looks not inside the bag"): the plastic's highlights and creases now run across the ring, a blank label panel says what the bag is, and it lies on a sage green desk pad, not the same honey desk as the badge beside it.
- Steel Safe (3: "A little too glossy"): matte paint; the Blender model is kept.
- Doorknob Print (3; set: "kinda meh as an object") becomes the Trench Coat on a wall hook: the genre's coat, beside its hat.
- Bloodhound (3: "Not sure if this could be an object"; set: "looks like a character") becomes the Handcuffs, on a coral red floor, modelled in Blender for the links.
- Squad Car (3, no comment) is unchanged.
- Fix round 2, 2026-11-08, after the producer's second grades (Detective 4 of 5; set: "Magnifier is a tad strange maybe?").
- Magnifier (3: "It's kinda elongated"): a short chunky handle, 0.30 long instead of 0.48, so the glass reads as one compact round object.
- Handcuffs (3: "The material is a little too metallic maybe, should be softer in texture"): soft satin grey steel in the plan and the model.
- Fix round 3, 2026-11-08, after the producer's third grades (Detective 5 of 5).
- Magnifier (1: "Too large the glass is, and the handle is too small"): round 2's long thin handle was "elongated" and round 3's short one too small. The handle is now 0.40 long and sturdy, about 0.85 of the lens's width from the rim as on the real Map card, and the blockout is framed at 0.9 so the glass is smaller on the card.
