## Notes

- Composed on 2026-11-08 from the outside rater's best variant (v3) after a review of all three pre-final variants and the producer's earlier grades (experiments/plan_review.py, runs/night/plan_review.json).
- Tea Cup: outside 3, as its twins in v1 and v2. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v3_01.
- Hat Box: outside 4, reworded because its ribbon and bow drew a gift box. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v3_02.
- Top Hat: outside 5, the best grade of the three variants; v2's grey hat was broken. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v3_03.
- Parasol: outside 3, as in v2. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v3_04.
- Pocket Watch replaces the Violin (outside 3), which names music, not the period. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v1_04.
- Folding Fan: ungraded; no graded alternative is better. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v3_06.
- Cake Stand: afternoon tea without a second cup. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v3_07.
- Steamer Trunk: reworded with a flat top and straps, because its rounded lid drew a treasure chest. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v3_08.
- Piano: the set's one place card. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v3_10.
- Colors rewritten under the rich-color rule adopted overnight (on the twins, closer to their real cards by CSD on 29 of 40): Tea Cup: pale blue, gold rim → rich cobalt blue, gold rim, white steam, color words in the subject to match; Hat Box: burgundy red with a cream cord handle → rich crimson red, cream cord handle, navy blue lid; Parasol: lavender canopy, white lace, wood brown handle → rich violet canopy, white lace, wood brown handle, color words in the subject to match; Pocket Watch: gold, white face → rich gold, white face, navy numerals; Folding Fan: red silk, gold ribs → rich red silk, gold ribs, plum pivot; Cake Stand: white, pink icing → white porcelain, pink icing, mint green icing; Steamer Trunk: wood brown with tan leather straps and brass fittings → rich red-brown wood, tan leather straps, brass fittings, forest green handles; Pearl Necklace: white pearls, gold clasp → white pearls, gold clasp, pale silver sheen, warm orange → rich pink; Piano: dark wood brown, ivory keys, brass gold → rich red-brown wood, ivory and black keys, brass gold pedals, color words in the subject to match.
- Kept by hand: the Top Hat stays black with a red band (outside 5; black is the object), and the piano keeps black keys.
- After the final draw, 2026-11-08 (runs/final3/period-drama): all nine Pearl Necklace drawings failed the style check (style 0.40-0.55, the set's lowest), a navy cushion filling the card, so it is swapped for the Quill Pen, the outside rater's 4, its inkwell's dark green made deep emerald green under the rich-color rule. Source: runs/prefinal/period-drama v1_06.
- Hat Box reworded with wide crimson and cream stripes: without its bow it drew as a plain red drum, and 8 of 9 drawings measured too little color, detail and edges on the subject.
- Fix round, 2026-11-08, after the producer's grades on experiments/final_review.py (Period Drama 4 of 5, mean 3.8).
- Top Hat: the producer picked candidate 4 instead of 3.
- Hat Box (5, but the set comment: "looks too american and probably would not be recognized easily"): crimson and cream stripes under a navy lid read as a flag. Now soft rose pink with a cream satin bow, and a straw bonnet on the lid says what the box holds.
- Pocket Watch (2: "slightly off-center of the card, should be more centered as the point of visual attention"): the chain arced out to the right and the framing centered the watch and chain together. The watch now hangs from a chain that leaves the top of the card, and the blockout frames the case alone (k.unframed).
- Folding Fan (3, no comment): the red silk fan reads Asian, like the hat box read American; now a pale pink European fan with a lace edge, on the same Blender model.
- Cake Stand and Piano (3, no comment) are unchanged: no comment says what to fix.
- Hat Box, redrawn pink with a bonnet on its lid, was still "just a strange object" to the producer (2026-11-08, after the fix round's draw). Replaced by the Candelabra, chosen by the producer from four proposals: the genre's manor-house object, unlike any other card in the set; modelled in Blender so the arms attach. Rich teal adds a hue the set lacks.
- Fix round 2, 2026-11-08, after the producer's second grades (Period Drama 4 of 5; set: "Fan maybe a little off, idk? And candelabra perspective?").
- Candelabra (2: "Perspective seems a little off?"; the producer picked candidate 8): every drawing showed the base from well above and the arms and cups straight on. The blockout now looks from the front at the height of its middle (8 degrees round, 6 up, 85 mm), so base and cups agree.
- Top Hat (3: "Maybe the rim is too curved on one side, noticeable"): all eight candidates curled the near side of the brim up and left the far side flat; the brim now curls evenly on both sides, seen from the front.
- Parasol (4: "could we rotate the parasol slightly?"): it leans 10 degrees instead of 20, so the lace on the right no longer bunches.
- Folding Fan (4, set: "a little off"): the model was rolled 14 degrees, so one outer stick lay flat and the other rose; the kept edit had also turned the lace into flat scallops. Now opened evenly with deeper pleats, a lace edge, and the model in the plan's pink and ivory.
- Cake Stand (3: "The white material is too glossy"): matte porcelain in the plan and the model.
- Fix round 3, 2026-11-08, after the producer's third grades (Period Drama 5 of 5).
- Top Hat (3 again, no comment): every candidate drawn from text, sixteen over two rounds, curled one side of the brim higher; now built in Blender with the brim curled evenly at both sides, seen almost from the front.
