## Notes

- Composed on 2026-11-08 from the outside rater's best variant (v1) after a review of all three pre-final variants and the producer's earlier grades (experiments/plan_review.py, runs/night/plan_review.json).
- Cowboy Hat: the producer's 5 in batch 2; the pre-final hat was graded broken. Source: runs/western-batch2 v1_01.
- Sheriff Star replaces the Gold Pan (outside 2): the genre's badge, absent from all three variants. It rose from 2 to 4 with a Blender model; no accent, since a red gem drew a jewel (lessons). Source: runs/western-blockout v1_05.
- Harmonica: the producer's 5 in batch 2, with that wording; on v1's pink waves, the set's one pink. Source: runs/western-batch2 v1_03.
- Bandana replaces the Barrel (the producer's 1): the producer's 5 in batch 1. Source: runs/western-batch v1_03.
- Cowboy Boots: a pair with spurs and no stitched stars, as the producer asked in batch 2 (5); outside 4 on v3's twin. Source: runs/prefinal/western v1_04.
- Saddle on a fence rail (v3) instead of on a hay bale (v1), which drew one stirrup and an awkward perch; built objects gained most from blockouts. Source: runs/prefinal/western v3_05.
- Cactus: outside 4, the producer's 4, 5 and 5 on earlier wordings. Source: runs/prefinal/western v1_06.
- Covered Wagon: outside 3, the producer's 4 and 3; four wheels, tongue on the ground. Source: runs/prefinal/western v1_08.
- Windmill with a solid tapering tower (the producer's 4 in batch 1) instead of v1's open truss, which the lessons advise against. Source: runs/western-batch v1_08.
- Campfire: the producer's 5 in batch 2, with v1's grey enamel pot instead of a black one. Source: runs/western-batch2 v1_09.
- Colors rewritten under the rich-color rule adopted overnight (on the twins, closer to their real cards by CSD on 29 of 40): Cowboy Hat: tan brown with a red band → rich caramel brown with a red band; Bandana: red with white dots and a small yellow knot → rich red cloth, white dots, small yellow knot; Cowboy Boots: leather brown with silver spurs and turquoise stitching → rich red-brown leather, gold stitching, silver spurs; Saddle: leather brown with silver stirrup trim → rich chestnut brown leather, golden wood fence rail, gold stitching, silver stirrup trim; Cactus: green with a pink flower → rich green with a pink flower; Covered Wagon: white canvas, wood brown, red wheel rims → white canvas, wood brown bed and tongue, red wheel rims; Windmill: grey tower and white blades with a red tail vane → rich red-brown tower, white blades, golden yellow tail vane; Campfire: orange flames, wood brown logs, grey enamel coffee pot with a red rim, grey stones → orange flames, wood brown logs, plum enamel coffee pot with a red rim, small grey stones, color words in the subject to match.
- Kept by hand: the Sheriff Star stays plain gold (tip colors drew a jewel before), the Harmonica keeps the producer's 5 colors, and the hat and cactus lose invented shading colors.
- Fix round, 2026-11-08, after the producer's grades on experiments/final_review.py (Western 4 of 5, mean 4.4).
- Windmill (3: "Looks like a monument. Maybe add a recognizable field of wheat beneath? Is the structure correct?"): no, a farm wind pump stands on an open lattice tower of four legs with cross braces, not a solid one; the solid tower came from the rule against trusses. Rebuilt in Blender with the lattice, in a wheat field.
- Campfire (set comment: "somewhat off"): the cabin and the large plum pot beside the fire are gone; the logs lean in a cone and a small blue enamel pot, the cowboy's, stands at the edge of the stones.
- Covered Wagon (set comment: "should have its own nature background"): from a striped backdrop to its own scene, a dirt trail across the prairie; the Blender model is kept.
- Fix round 2, 2026-11-08, after the producer's second grades (Western 5 of 5).
- Campfire (3: "Maybe the kettle is too metallic in texture?"): the pot is soft matte enamel.
