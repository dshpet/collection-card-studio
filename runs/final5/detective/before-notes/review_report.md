## Art director

The generated cards are close to the real ones. The magnifier could almost pass. Its main tell is the lens taking on the backdrop green and blending in. The handcuffs are the weaker card: plain grey and low in contrast on an empty pink field, much quieter than the loud, saturated real cards. Most specialists picked the wrong card in p01. On the controls they flagged traits the real cards have: neon trims, mesh, outlines, dark water, bubbles, warm stone and busy paving. Those are part of the house style, so do not correct them away. Leave the style text unchanged.

1. **Handcuffs (p01 B) are a quiet, washed-out card. Grey steel sits on salmon pink at almost the same lightness, there is no dark anchor and no saturated colour on the hero, and a small object floats in a lot of empty field. Next to the real cards (neon sneakers, red and white hoop, magenta jellyfish) it looks muted and timid. That is the strongest tell in this set.** Trust value_contrast and composition: this matches the card. Overrule the other specialists who passed B as clean real hardware. Fix it through finish and value, not size or view. Make the steel a bright polished chrome with crisp highlights and cool reflections, and make the floor slightly richer. Do not repeat the lost satin-and-darker-shadow note.
2. **In the magnifier (p02 A), the lens glass repeats the green rings behind it, so the lens reads as a hole and blends into the backdrop. The card is flat in value apart from the red handle.** Trust value_contrast. Overrule shape_proportions: the handle form cannot be changed. Viewpoint and composition calls are weak, so leave the view alone. Use the pattern of the earlier kept Magnifier note: tint the glass, darken one side slightly, add a faint shadow.
3. **Many specialist 'generated patterns' come from real cards. They named the neon multicolour trims, mesh texture and hard floor/wall seam (real sneakers), heavy dark outlines (real hoop), warm cream stone, striped wallpaper and paving bricks (real fountain), and the low-key dark water, bubbles, light rays and outlines (real jellyfish). The real game is loud, saturated, detailed and sometimes outlined.** Overrule these. Do not push the generator toward muted colour, smooth surfaces, no outlines or plain backgrounds. That would over-correct away from the real cards.

- p01: Handcuffs: Paint the steel as bright, polished chrome with crisp white highlights along the top of each ring and cool blue reflections inside the rings, so the cuffs read clearly darker and brighter than the floor. Make the pink floor slightly richer and more saturated coral, with a soft lighter glow just behind the cuffs. Keep the cuffs, the chain, the locks, the keyholes and the view.
- p02: Magnifier: Tint the lens glass a pale, clear cool blue-grey in place of the background green, with one soft curved reflection along its upper left. Make the right side of the metal ring and the underside of the red handle slightly darker, and add a soft faint shadow of the magnifier on the rings. Keep the magnifier, the green ring backdrop and the view.
