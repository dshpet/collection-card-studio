## Art director

Not yet. The cards share the real cards' genre and subject, but only p01 could pass. As a set they read as a fatter, glossier, louder toy version of the game. The key shows the root cause: generated cards overshoot on saturation, gloss, chubbiness and props, and the real cards are the quieter, more realistic ones.

Overruled: color_palette, value_contrast, lighting_shading and surface_finish mostly called the real card the odd one and asked for more gloss, more saturation and rounder toy forms. Those fixes would make the problem worse. I kept only their observed differences and reversed the direction. Examples are the camera, teacup, tent and cauldron calls.

Also overruled: edges_linework on p01–p07, where the fine detail it flags belongs to the real cards. composition_hierarchy on hero size for p03, p04, p05, p07, p08 and p09, where the real heroes are the smaller ones. shape_proportions on p01 and p02, and viewpoint_depth on p07, where the real barrel has the thin ellipse.

Trusted: staging_detail, shape_proportions on most pairs, and the p10–p12 scene calls.

The new style text avoids overshooting the other way: the real cards are still stylized and colorful, so muting stays slight.

1. **Candy-toy color: generated cards use louder, more saturated heroes and backgrounds than the real twins, with an orange or cyan glow behind the hero and purple or red tinted shadows. Real cards use natural local color: tan glove, brown barrel, mustard taxi, gray-beige computer, ochre tent, gray road. Their backgrounds are calmer and often darken at the corners. Seen on p02, p04, p05, p06, p07, p08, p09, p10, p11, p12 and mildly p01.** Paint each material its natural color, slightly muted: tan leather, brown wood, gray metal, off-white ceramic. Use calm backgrounds with slightly darker corners and neutral soft shadows. Keep hues clean and readable, as the real cards do.
2. **Inflated shapes: parts are swollen like toys. Fat handles and rims, bulging wheel arches, a huge lens, pillow-soft boxes, oversized tassels. Real cards are slimmer, with flat faces, thin rims and believable proportions. Seen on p02, p03, p04, p05, p07, p08, p09, p12.** Draw true proportions: slim rims and handles, modest lenses and wheels, flat side faces, crisp small bevels, thin bands and cords. Keep the gentle softness of the real cards, not a sharp technical render.
3. **Plastic finish with no fine detail: everything is smooth, glossy and chunky, with big sticker highlights. Real cards carry fine material detail and quieter sheen: lens knurling, thin stitching, wood grain, apple speckle, soot on iron, twisted cord strands. Seen on all cards, worst on p02, p03, p04, p06, p07, p08, p12.** Use satin surfaces with small soft highlights. Add fine real detail: grain, thin stitching, ribbing, speckle, gentle wear. Metal and glass keep a modest shine.
4. **Invented props turn scene cards into busy dioramas: lantern, bedroll and flowers on the tent; island, life ring and bollard on the lighthouse; crate, stump and rock ring on the cauldron. Real scene cards show the subject in a quiet setting of a few large soft shapes. The current negative wording did not stop this.** Show only the named subject in a quiet setting: plain ground and a few large soft background shapes. Background detail stays soft and low in contrast.
5. **Raised, forced camera: generated cards look down at the subject or push parts toward the viewer. Open barrel top, wide cup rim, high computer view, a bugle bell ballooning close to the lens. Real cards use a lower, calmer view with thin rim ellipses and mild perspective.** Use a low camera near the subject's mid-height with mild perspective. All parts stay at consistent scale, and round openings appear as thin ellipses.
6. **Hero overscaled: on object cards the generated hero is pushed larger toward the edges. Real cards leave open background around it (bugle, camera, cup, taxi). Exception: the real lighthouse is a tight crop, so framing can vary.** Draw the object at natural size with open background on all sides, sitting comfortably inside the card.

Draw the real object with true proportions, only lightly stylized: slim parts, flat faces, crisp edges. Leave open background around it. Low camera, mild perspective.

Soft directional light, neutral shadows, a calm background with slightly darker corners.
Only the named object, in a quiet setting of few large soft shapes. No text, frame or border.
