## Art director

The cards are fairly close. The single-object icon cards (Saturn, helmet, flask, UFO) are near a pass on rendering and backdrop, and Saturn and the helmet are kept. Three things give the batch away: framing (small, centred, whole heroes in empty space), invented clutter with gritty photographic ground on the scene cards (rover, rocket, control panel, cow and rooster), and uniform chrome or lacquer with rivet-row micro detail. Flat frontal light comes next, and colour is a minor issue. I overruled several specialist calls. All of shape_proportions is overruled because form cannot be judged across different objects. On the p02 and p07 controls, I discarded the picks from the viewpoint, lighting, colour, surface, edges and staging specialists. On p10, the shape, edges and viewpoint specialists picked the real vending machine. In the edit notes I changed only finish, light, colour and extras. I did not reframe anything, because framing changes were never kept in past rounds.

1. **Hero shown small, whole and centred, with an even margin of empty backdrop or ground around it (satellite, rover, UFO, rocket, flask, star chart). Real cards push the hero close so it fills most of the card height and often run it off a card edge. This is the strongest tell across the set. The composition, staging, viewpoint and value reports all describe this one cause.** Draw the hero large and close, filling about three quarters of the card height, in a slight three-quarter view from a little above. Let one part of it run off a card edge where the object allows.
2. **Invented setting clutter and photographic ground: pebble-strewn regolith (rover), a gritty tiled concrete plaza (rocket), a porthole, light strip and rivets on the wall (control panel), and a UFO with a light beam (cow and rooster). Real backdrops are a few large, quiet, smooth shapes.** Set the hero on one or two smooth, softly shaded backdrop shapes with no pebbles, joints, grain or added props.
3. **Equally shiny chrome or lacquer over the whole object, with hot white streaks and repeated crisp micro detail: rivet rows, identical buttons, fine panel grids, chrome spacesuits. Real cards are softer painted surfaces that mix matte and gloss and keep fine detail sparse.** Paint surfaces soft and satin, with one broad highlight per form and only a few small, quiet details.
4. **Light is frontal and even. Side faces barely darken, and cast shadows point one way while the object looks lit from another (rocket, rover, panel). Real cards use a clear upper-left key with a darker right side.** Light from the upper left so the right side of the hero is clearly darker, with soft contact shadow under it.
5. **Colour: the hero drops to near-colourless grey metal while the backdrop stays loud (rover, UFO, spacesuits), or the backdrop hue tints the hero's whites and glass (pink flask). This matters less than the issues above.** Give metal and white heroes a clear local colour or warm trim, and keep their whites cool neutral rather than tinted by the backdrop.

- p01: keep
- p03: Satellite: Make the gold box a satin painted gold with one soft broad highlight instead of mirror chrome and white streaks, and make its right side face clearly darker. Make the solar panels soft matte blue with fewer, softer cell lines. Keep the green rays.
- p04: Computer (rover): Replace the gritty grey gravel with a smooth, softly shaded dusty warm-grey ground with no pebbles or rocks. Give the white body orange trim panels. Light from the front left so the right side of the body and the inner wheel faces are clearly darker.
- p05: Plane (UFO): Remove the rivets on the hull. Make the rim lights soft warm yellow dots that cast a small warm glow onto the hull next to them. Light from the upper left so the right side of the saucer is slightly darker. Keep the sky and clouds.
- p06: Map (star chart): Make the disc face a slightly lighter medium navy blue with a soft glow across its upper half. Paint the constellation lines and stars as softer, slightly glowing marks, and drop the thin grid circles. Light the rim brightly on the upper-left edge.
- p08: keep
- p09: Control Panel: Remove the porthole, the ceiling light strip and the wall rivets and seams, leaving a plain smooth purple wall slightly darker than the panel. Light from the upper left so the panel casts a soft shadow onto the wall at the lower right. Keep the panel as is.
- p10: Vending (flask): Make the glass clear pale grey-blue with white edge highlights instead of pink-tinted. Make the left side of the glass and the liquid slightly darker, with the liquid a slightly deeper yellow-green. Keep the pink background.
- p11: Rocket: Make the ground a smooth, softly shaded grey launch pad with no paving joints or grain. Remove the rivet row under the nose cone. Light from the upper left to match the cast shadow, so the right half of the body and the right faces of the fins are clearly darker.
- p12: Hitchhike (cow and rooster): Remove the flying saucer and its light beam and leave plain sky. Make the spacesuits soft matte white fabric with a few gentle folds and only a small sheen, keeping the orange trim. Make the grass a slightly smoother, even green with fewer tufts.
