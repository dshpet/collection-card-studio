## Art director

Close, but not there yet. The set matches the real cards' backdrops, framing and general rendering, and the broom and pumpkin could pass as they are. Eight cards need small edits, mainly the cat, house, coffin and tombstone. The main tells are the daytime moons, hueless black heroes, over-applied surface texture and too little cool shadow.

The specialists were misaligned in places: lighting_shading swapped p08 and p09, and surface_finish described the house under p07 and the cat under p08. I read those comments against the cards they actually describe.

I overruled these points:
- All proportion, thickness and roundness fixes, because form cannot be judged across objects.
- Staging, lighting and surface tells that also appear on real cards: curved decks, ring ripples, cracked planks, pebbles, brick rings, grass tufts, pale sunbursts and glossy mid-tone finishes. The real p04 ship's wheel, p12 buoy, p11 duck and p07 fountain all have some of these, so busy ground and small props are not tells. The p04 and p12 calls on those controls were false positives.
- The composition specialist's pick of the real lamp as odd, and its crop-and-enlarge notes.
- The lighting and subject-theme calls on the broom and pumpkin, which I kept because the evidence was weak and partly theme-driven.

The value and color specialists conflicted on the candelabra background, one asking for lighter and the other for quieter. I settled on slightly lighter and slightly calmer.

1. **A cratered moon sits in a bright daytime sky on the haunted house and the coffin. It is an invented second focal point that contradicts the daylight, and no real card does this.** Remove the moon. Leave plain blue sky with soft white clouds.
2. **Dark heroes fall to a hueless black mass. The witch hat, cauldron and cat lose hue in the shadows and have few middle values, while the real navy cap stays blue all the way into its shadow. This is one root cause, reported separately as low contrast, low subject chroma and weak form light.** Paint black objects as a deep colour: plum, indigo or blue iron. Keep a lighter lit top plane with a soft fade into coloured shadow, and use true black only in the deepest contact shadow.
3. **One applied skin covers the object: flocked grain on the cat, evenly spaced pits on the tombstone, outlined raised blocks on the house, insistent grain on the coffin, speckle on the cauldron belly. Real cards have quieter surfaces, with larger smooth areas.** Quiet the surface: thin faint seams, fine sparse wear, and large smooth areas with broad soft highlights.
4. **Lit and shadow sides sit too close together on the house, coffin and tombstone, and bright edges run around the forms. Real cards such as the lighthouse and stone table have a clear lit side against a cool blue-violet shadow side.** Pick one light side. Let the far side fall into cool blue-violet shadow, with a firm dark contact shadow.
5. **The candelabra's brass is uniform wet gold with crisp dark streaks, on a loud, dark violet field. The card reads as two masses and looks spotlit, where the real lamp has softer, browner brass and a lighter centre.** Use muted satin brass with broad soft reflections, and a slightly lighter, slightly calmer violet field with a pale centre.
6. **The crystal ball's smoke is feathered, hair-like streaks with star sparkles. This is a generic effect that looks unlike the clean, flat translucent glass on the real drink card.** Replace the streaks with smooth soft mist and leave out the sparkles.

- p01: Change the black hat to a deep plum charcoal, slightly lighter on the top planes of the cone and brim. Let the shadows under the brim stay rich purple.
- p02: Paint the cauldron as deep blue iron with one smooth, broad soft sheen and no speckle. Add a warm orange glow from the flame on the underside of the belly. Make the paving a slightly lighter, cool neutral grey with fewer, fainter seams.
- p03: keep
- p05: Make the brass muted satin with broad soft reflections and slightly browner darks, replacing the crisp dark streaks. Make the violet background slightly lighter and calmer, with a pale centre. Let warm candlelight fall softly on the cups and arms.
- p06: Replace the feathered streaks inside the ball with smooth soft mist. Remove the star sparkles and tiny dots. Keep the glass rim, stand and shelf as they are.
- p07: Replace the sandy felt grain with short smooth fur that has a soft sheen on the head and back. Change the black to a deep indigo charcoal with broad lighter planes on the head, chest and back. Keep true black only in the pupils and contact shadow.
- p08: Remove the moon and leave plain blue sky with the clouds. Make the stone matte, with flatter blocks and thin, quiet mortar lines. Light the house from the left, with the right side in cool blue-violet shadow and warm window light on the stone around each frame.
- p09: Remove the moon and leave plain blue sky. Change the wood to a slightly deeper, muted walnut with fine, faint grain and no knots. Darken the front side to a cool shadow and give the cast shadow on the grass a crisper edge.
- p10: Replace the evenly spaced pits with hard matte stone showing fine, irregular wear, and make the moss finer. Darken the left side firmly, with a darker recess in the cross and a dark contact line under the base. Calm the bright yellow glow behind the stone to an even orange wall.
- p11: keep
