## Art director

Close. Materials, rendering softness, backdrops and the level of scene staging already match the real cards, and the hat could pass as is. Three habits still give the generated cards away. First, a warm hero on a warm backdrop: the boots and saloon are worst, then the cactus and lasso. Second, flat even light with a weak shadow side: the star, cactus, saddle and boots. Third, stitching and grain detail on leather: the saddle and boots. A secondary tell is centered, frontal posing, which edit notes cannot change. The controls (p05, p12) drew confident wrong picks from the lighting, edges, surface and staging specialists, so their claims about outlines, pebbles, glows, trusses, droplets and scenes were overruled. The edit notes are small and touch only color, light, finish and extras.

1. **Warm-on-warm palette. The hero, the ground and the backdrop sit in one orange family: boots on orange stripes, the saloon on orange desert, the cactus on an orange sunburst, the tan lasso on tan sand. Real cards put a warm hero on a cool backdrop (coral flip-flops on teal, red mower on cyan, yellow duck on green, umbrella on cyan). This is the strongest tell in the set.** Put warm objects on a cool backdrop (teal, sky blue, green or violet) and keep the ground paler and cooler than the hero, so the hero is the warmest shape on the card.
2. **Flat, even front light. Both sides of the hero get nearly the same light, the shadow side is shallow, and cast shadows are weak (sheriff star, cactus, saddle, boots, saloon front). Real cards commit to one key light from the upper side, with a clearly darker side and a firm cast shadow, like the duck, the umbrella underside and the dock posts. Overruled where this specialist flagged real cards (jacket, fruit plate, guitar).** Light the hero from the upper left. Make the right side clearly darker and drop a firm shadow down and to the right onto the ground or backdrop.
3. **Fine leather detail. Dashed stitch lines along every edge and pebbled pore grain show on the saddle and the boots, the same habit as the generated glove in the lessons. Real leather and fabric cards stay smooth with broad soft highlights. Other surface-noise calls are overruled: the real fruit plate has droplets, the real gas pump has scuffs and the real lifebuoy has rope texture.** Draw leather as smooth, soft material with broad gentle highlights and at most a few solid decorative lines.
4. **Grey metal hero with no light structure, sitting between a loud magenta backdrop and an orange shelf (horseshoe). A gold star with no shadow side sits on a hot magenta glow (sheriff star). The backdrop does all the color work.** Give metal a clear bright top face and a darker side face, and calm saturated magenta backdrops slightly toward violet-pink.
5. **Centered, frontal or side-on, symmetric poses (star, cactus, campfire, boots, hat). This is a real but secondary tell. Pose is outside what edit notes may change, so it is not put into notes this round.** No edit-note action. Flag for the next generation round: prefer a turned three-quarter view.
6. **Overruled: dark outlines (edges mostly flagged real cards), scattered pebbles (the real duck card has them), glow behind the hero (the real jacket and umbrella have it), wide desert scenes with mesas (the real gas pump has one), stage trusses (the real guitar has them), cream canvas drift (the real caravan is cream), and every shape or thickness call (form cannot be judged across objects). The control pairs p05 and p12 drew several confident wrong picks, so those dimensions are noisy here.** No change.

- p01: keep
- p02: Tone the lime backdrop down to a softer mid green. Make the sand slightly paler and cooler beige so the tan rope stands out from it. Darken the center of the coil with a deeper shadow.
- p03: Light the star from the upper left: darker gold on the lower-right points and the lower arc of the ring, with a soft cast shadow down-right. Calm the magenta slightly toward violet-pink and remove the bright pink glow behind the badge.
- p04: Change the sunburst to a cool teal or sky blue. Light the cactus from the upper left with a clearly darker green right side. Add a firm contact shadow on the sand.
- p06: Shade the horseshoe as warm-grey iron with a bright top face and a clearly darker side face, plus one broad soft highlight in place of the brushed streaks. Make the magenta backdrop slightly softer and less saturated.
- p07: Let the fire cast warm orange light onto the underside of the pot, the inner faces of the logs and the stones. Make the pot dark grey with a lit rim instead of solid black. Keep the scene otherwise.
- p08: Paint the saloon in slightly weathered grey-brown wood with muted cream trim, and make the ground a paler, cooler sand. Light from the upper left, with the right wall in shadow and a deep shadow under the porch roof. Make the wood grain fainter and sparser.
- p09: Remove the sun disc and the daisies. Make the grass a slightly smoother, even green with fewer blades. Keep the wagon as is.
- p10: Make the saddle smooth leather with no dashed stitching and no pore grain. Light from the upper left, with a darker right flap and the saddle's shadow cast onto the beam. Turn the pad a slightly more muted red.
- p11: Change the background stripes to cool teal or sky blue. Replace the dotted turquoise stitching with a few soft solid lines. Light from the upper left so each shaft has a broad lighter face and a darker side.
