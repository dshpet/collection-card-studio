## Art director

Close. Most generated cards could sit in the real set: the saddle, wagon, boots and campfire pass as they are. The biggest tell across the batch is the camera: heroes are dead-on, symmetric and centered, while the real cards turn to three-quarter view and crowd an edge. Edit notes can't fix that, so it needs a regeneration pass. Beyond that, two cards clearly miss on their own. The saloon has dull grey grain-textured wood and a busy prop-filled desert. The horseshoe reads as beige clay, not steel. The rest need only light touches: calm the glow behind the hero (cactus, hat), give the star a shadow side, and make the lasso rope matte. Overruled: all form, thickness and roundness calls from shape_proportions, which were also wrong on p12. The inked-outline claim from edges_linework doesn't show on the cards. Staging_detail picked real cards in p03, p06 and p08. Lighting_shading's 'near-black' wagon shadows are overstated. Several value and colour calls on the controls (p01, p09) flagged real cards.

1. **Camera and pose: generated heroes are shown dead-on, mirror-symmetric, centered and whole, with even margins (cactus, sheriff star, horseshoe, saloon, campfire, hat; saddle and boots in flat side profile). Most real cards turn the object to a three-quarter view from a slightly raised camera and let it reach or cross one or two card edges (fountain, sneakers, boat, dock posts, phone booth, lawnmower, umbrella). This gives the set away more than anything else, but an edit note can't fix it. It has to come from regeneration.** When regenerating: turn the object about 30° into a three-quarter view from a slightly raised camera, so a little of its top and of the ground under it shows. Make it larger so it reaches close to one or two card edges. Keep the backdrop the same.
2. **Saloon: this is the one card that reads as a different product. The wood is a dull grey-brown with fine grain on every board, the frame is filled with small hard-edged extras (fences, shrubs, rocks, scattered pebbles, a step board), and the colour is pulled flat and dusty. Real full-scene cards (the station clock, the gas pump) use saturated local colour and broad, smooth surfaces.** Paint the wood a clear, saturated warm brown with broad smooth planks. Make the ground one smooth sandy plane, and keep only the distant mesas and sky.
3. **Wrong material reading on metal: the horseshoe looks like matte beige-grey clay tinted by the magenta wall and orange table, not steel. Real metal (the lawnmower handle, the clock rim, the pump nozzle) is cool grey with a clean bright highlight.** Cool steel grey with bluish shadows and one crisp, bright highlight along the top edge.
4. **A bright glow patch right behind the hero (the white burst behind the cactus, the hot pink spot behind the horseshoe, the near-white ray centre behind the hat). Real ray and ring fields do brighten toward the centre, but gently, and they keep darker corners (the jacket, the duck).** Spread the backdrop light evenly. The centre is only slightly lighter, and the corners are slightly darker.
5. **Orange and tan western props (hat, lasso, boots, saddle) are painted in one hue, with shadows that only darken the same orange. The real boat shifts its hull shadow to red-maroon. This is a mild tell, because the real cards also use saturated orange wood.** Shift the deepest shadows slightly toward reddish or plum brown. Keep the lit colour as it is.
6. **Even, symmetrical front lighting, with the same hot spot repeated on every part (the star's points and ring balls, every twist of the lasso rope), so the main object has no shadow side.** Light from the upper left so the lower-right side turns gently darker. Keep one or two soft highlights.

- p02: Calm the bright white glow behind the cactus so the cyan rays brighten only slightly toward the centre. Slightly soften and lighten the cast shadow on the sand.
- p03: keep
- p04: Paint the horseshoe as cool steel grey with bluish shadows and one crisp bright highlight along the top edge. Make the pink wall an even tone with no glow spot behind the horseshoe. Soften the dark cast shadow on the table slightly.
- p05: Light the star from the upper left so the lower-right arms are slightly darker. Keep bright highlights on only one or two ring balls. Darken the purple rings slightly toward the card corners.
- p06: keep
- p07: keep
- p08: Make the blue rays even, with only a slightly lighter centre and slightly darker corners, instead of the near-white burst behind the hat. Shade the crown dent and the brim underside slightly toward a plum-brown.
- p10: Paint the saloon in a saturated warm brown wood with broad smooth planks and no fine grain. Remove the fences, shrubs, rocks and scattered pebbles, and leave smooth sand. Keep the distant mesas and sky.
