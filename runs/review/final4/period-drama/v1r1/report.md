## Art director

Close. Two of the three generated cards pass or nearly pass. The hatbox reads as a real card, and most specialists picked the real sneakers over it. The pocket watch gives itself away only through its even frontal light and its all-over mirror gloss on the gold. The fan is the clear outlier: realistic linen weave and lace, flat front light with no shadow side, and pale pink on pale blue with almost no value separation. Its note fixes finish, light and backdrop value. Overruled: findings on the real control cards (the cap's room, the book's sticker outlines, the dock's props), the ring box and sneakers called generated, and any proportion or framing changes.

1. **Real-world fine texture and craft detail instead of the game's smooth broad parts: linen weave and finely drawn lace on the fan, woven straw and a petal-by-petal rose on the bonnet. This is the clearest giveaway on the fan.** Paint cloth, paper and straw as smooth, broad colour panels with a soft sheen. Draw lace and trims as one smooth band with a few large, simple scallops.
2. **Flat, frontal, even light with no clear shadow side and little contact shadow. The fan's pleats alternate evenly and it sits on the backdrop like a sticker. The watch is lit evenly from the front. The real ring box and dock show a clear lit side and a soft shadow side.** Light from the upper left. Make the right side of the object gently darker and put a soft shadow under it.
3. **Pale hero on a pale backdrop: the fan, its lace and its ribs sit in the same light band as the light-blue waves, so only hue separates them. Real cards keep the hero clearly apart from the backdrop in value.** Give the hero slightly deeper shadow tones, and make the backdrop a medium tone so the hero reads in black and white.
4. **Uniform mirror gloss over the whole metal part: the watch case, bow and crown all carry one bright lacquered band. Real cards mix matte and shiny parts and keep highlights narrow.** Make gold satin, with one narrow highlight along the lit edge and soft warm mid-tones elsewhere.
5. **Overruled.
