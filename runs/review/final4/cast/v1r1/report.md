## Art director

Close. The detective card (p04 B) could pass as real. The barn (p01 B) and ballroom (p05 B) cards match the real cards in characters and staging, but their floors are too busy: straw drawn strand by strand, and many sharp glints on the parquet. A small floor-texture edit, plus a slight cooling of the warm wash, should fix them. Most of the framing, lighting and shape findings are overruled, because the control pairs show that front-on, whole-object, staged compositions are real-game features.

1. **Fine, busy texture over the whole floor: B in p01 has straw drawn strand by strand, and B in p05 has a parquet floor covered in streaky white mirror glints. The real cards keep the ground as a few large, quiet shapes with soft sheen. Staging_detail, surface_finish, value_contrast and edges_linework all describe this one problem.** Per card: draw the floor as broad, smooth surfaces with a few soft clumps or one gentle blurred reflection.
2. **Warm wash. The whole set leans amber: red barn wall plus orange straw in p01, and an amber floor under a cream cow in p05. White wool and steel pick up a cream cast. This is real but mild, because the real cards are also warm and saturated.** Per card, slightly: keep whites and steel neutral, and make the floor a little less orange than the characters.
3. **Overruled. The camera is pulled back to show full figures or whole objects on a stage, pedestal or floor, square to the viewer and front-lit with a centred spotlight. Both control pairs contradict this: the ship's wheel on its pedestal and the drum kit on its spotlit stage are real cards. Full-body framing and frontal staging are part of the game's look and are not tells.** No change. Do not crop in, re-angle, add side lighting or remove stages.
4. **Overruled. Some specialists said every generated card has a uniform dark outline around each form. The generated cards don't show this any more than the real ones do. Where outlines appear, they match the real cards. Shape and proportion calls are out of scope across different objects.** No change.

- p01: Shopping: Replace the strand-by-strand straw floor with a smooth golden straw mat with a few large, soft clumps. Paint the barn wall a slightly more muted dusty red with a few broad, plain boards. Keep the sheep, the cow, the milk can, the notepad and the view.
- p04: keep
- p05: Selfie: Replace the streaky mirror reflections on the floor with smooth, softly polished honey wood that has one gentle, blurred reflection under the dancers. Make the green curtains slightly lighter and softer. Keep the sheep, the cow, the bow, the bonnet and the view.
