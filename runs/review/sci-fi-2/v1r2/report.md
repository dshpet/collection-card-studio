## Art director

Close. Four of the ten generated cards could pass: the UFO, rocket and planet as they are, and the helmet after a slight color fix. The rest give themselves away mainly through invented sci-fi settings (lab props, riveted floors, clouds over the moon, sparkle stars, mirror floors), then through screws, rivets and gloss everywhere, then through whites and metals tinted by the backdrop. Small, centered framing is also a real giveaway, but card notes can't fix it. Overruled: every shape_proportions fix, because form can't be judged across different objects. Also every call against the real controls (p01 bottle and map, p08 scarf and book), and value_contrast's call against the real submarine (p05 B): its dark corners are real style, not tenebrism. Camera-angle fixes are left out of the notes.

1. **Invented sci-fi settings and effects around the hero. The pod has a lab full of consoles, a glowing coil tube and neon floor rings. The alien stands on riveted steel plates under a spotlight. The moon rover has a cloudy blue sky over its craters. The station has twinkle sparkle stars, and the control panel has a mirror floor reflection. Real cards use a flat sunburst, rings or gradient, or a setting made of a few large, quiet shapes (street, desk, dock). This is the biggest giveaway.** Backdrop is a plain sunburst, ring field or soft gradient, or one simple surface such as a matte floor or wooden ledge. Nothing in it but the hero and its soft contact shadow.
2. **Small mechanical detail and even gloss over the whole object: screws, rivets, panel seams, fine solar-cell grids, tyre treads, and a hot white spot on every bulb, joint and rim. Real cards keep surfaces soft, with a few broad shapes and one gentle sheen. The edges specialist's 'dark inked rings' point to the same cause (button rings, eye rings, bead outlines).** Smooth painted surfaces with two or three large seams at most, soft satin sheen, and one soft highlight per object.
3. **Neutral materials pick up the background hue. The station is all blue, the helmet shell turns peach on orange, and the pod's steel turns violet on purple. The card falls into one hue family, and on the robot and rover the hero sits in the same value band as the backdrop. Real cards keep whites and metals cool and neutral, with a clear hero-versus-background color split.** White and metal parts stay clean cool white or neutral grey, with blue-grey shadow sides that read clearly against the backdrop.
4. **Framing: the robot, alien, rover, pod and station sit small, centered and face-on, with an even margin of background around them. Real cards crop the hero big and close, at a slight 3/4 turn. Card notes cannot change this, but it is a real giveaway for the prompt layer.** Hero large and close, filling most of the card, at a gentle three-quarter turn.
5. **Weak key light. The robot and pod are lit flat from the front with no clear shadow side. This is a minor issue and mostly follows from the color problem above.** One soft key light from the upper left, with a clearly darker right side.

- p02: keep
- p03: Clean warm-white station hull with light grey shading and smooth modules without screws; solar panels in deep blue with a few broad cells; plain deep-blue space with a few tiny soft star dots.
- p04: Shade the robot's right side slightly cooler blue-grey; make the stripes a slightly deeper golden orange; matte wooden ledge without the glossy reflection.
- p05: keep
- p06: Soft contact shadow on a matte floor in place of the mirror reflection; satin teal finish instead of hard white edge highlights; no corner screws.
- p07: Plain soft lavender wall and simple matte round platform, with no consoles, coil tube or neon rings; pod shell in neutral brushed grey with no rivets; a few dim warm lights on the rim.
- p09: keep
- p10: Replace the cloudy sky with a smooth deep-blue space gradient; cool blue-grey ground with two or three large shallow craters; smooth orange deck without screws.
- p11: Slightly cooler white helmet shell with light blue-grey shading on the right side; visor slightly darker amber-gold than the background.
- p12: One plain smooth cool-grey floor without plate seams or rivets; even warm pink backdrop with no spotlight or dark corners.
