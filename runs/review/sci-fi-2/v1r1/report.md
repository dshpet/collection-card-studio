## Art director

Close in rendering, material softness and palette energy. The planet, rocket and UFO already pass next to the real ring and sunburst cards. The main tell is staging: the generator invents sci-fi environments (riveted floors, a neon lab, crater fields, starfields) where real cards use quiet gradients, rays, rings or a wooden ledge. Second, metal heroes either take on the background hue or stay a colorless grey. Fixing the backdrops and giving each hero a local color should make most of the redrawn cards pass. The proportion complaints are excluded and need no style change.

1. **Invented sci-fi settings give the generated cards away. Examples are riveted steel floor plates (robot, alien), a neon lab with consoles, a glowing tube and a ring pedestal (pod), a pebble-strewn crater landscape (rover) and a sparkle starfield with a planet in the corner (station). The real cards use quiet settings: a plain gradient, a sunburst, concentric rings, a simple wood ledge or desk, or a few large calm scenery shapes.** Stage each object on one quiet setting: a plain soft gradient, even sunburst rays, even concentric rings, or a simple wooden ledge or desk. Keep background surfaces smooth and soft, with only a few large shapes.
2. **Metal heroes lose their own color. Silver turns into the background hue (the purple pod, the blue station, the purple-tinted UFO), or the hero is a dead grey with no local color (the console, the rover). Real heroes always carry a clear local hue that contrasts with the background, as with the blue vending machine on purple and the yellow submarine on green.** Give the hero a saturated painted color that contrasts with the background, and keep the metal trim neutral grey.
3. **Several generated heroes are lit flat from the front, with a weak shadow side (robot, alien, pod, station). Real cards have upper-left light, with the right side turning to a deeper, cooler shadow.** Light the hero from the upper left so that its right side turns into a slightly deeper, cooler shadow, with a soft contact shadow underneath.
4. **Crisp, dense micro-detail on sci-fi objects: rivets, screws, panel seams, vent slits and fine solar-cell grids (pod, console, rover, station). Real cards keep fine marks sparse and soft.** Draw only a few soft panel seams, with very few rivets or screws.
5. **Overruled claims. All shape and proportion fixes are excluded, because form can't be judged across different objects. Value_contrast's p02 call (B, real) and p03 claim against the submarine are wrong, and so is lighting's p06 call (B, real), because those are real cards. Calls on the planet, rocket and UFO were weak (confidence 2–3) and could not be confirmed on the cards, which already match the ring and sunburst cards closely. The 'glow behind the helmet' is overruled: the real jacket's sunburst has the same light center.** No change.
