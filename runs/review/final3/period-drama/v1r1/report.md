## Art director

The generated cards are close to the real ones. 5 of the 10 could pass as they are: the watch, the fan, the top hat, the teacup and the parasol. The other 5 give themselves away mainly through the scene around the hero, not the hero itself. The surfaces look photographic (velvet, linen, flagstones, mahogany), the colors stay dark or in one hue, and on the hatbox the light is flat with no clear shadow side. The edit notes change only the backdrop, finish and light, and keep each object's form.

1. **Photographic surfaces in staged scenes. The fake cards render the ground and backdrop as real material: velvet pile with tufts (pearls), woven linen (cake stand), gritty grey flagstones with cracks (trunk), and lacquered mahogany grain with mirror floor reflections (piano). Real staged cards (dock, map on planks, fountain paving, suitcase floor) paint surfaces as smooth, soft, simplified shapes with only faint texture. This is the biggest giveaway because it covers the largest area of each card.** Paint floors, cloths and walls as smooth, softly shaded painted surfaces with faint, quiet texture and broad soft reflections.
2. **Uniform gloss and busy small hardware on the hero: equal lacquer on porcelain, wood and gold, stitched straps, rivets, eyelet lace. Real cards mix matte and satin parts and keep hardware broad and plain. This is visible on the trunk, piano and cake stand, and mildly on the parasol and watch.** Give the hero a mostly satin or matte finish, with highlights only on rims and top edges and plain broad hardware.
3. **One-hue, low-key or all-warm color plans: the navy-on-navy pearl card, the dark purple wall over the white cloth, and the all-warm red piano on cream and orange. Real cards usually set a clear hero against a contrasting colored field. Overruled: the value specialist's claim that real cards never use dark backgrounds. The real diploma card has a deep navy backdrop, so the fix is hue contrast and a lighter key, not banning dark tones.** Set a warm hero on a cool field or a cool hero on a warm field, with the backdrop mid-to-light and one clear hue.
4. **Weak, undirected light on simple heroes. The hatbox has one even sheen and no core shadow, and the pearls each carry an identical centered dot. Real cards commit to an upper-left key light with a clear darker right side.** Light from the upper left, with a clearly darker right side and a firm contact shadow.
5. **Overruled specialist calls. Shape, viewpoint and composition notes about proportions, camera angle and framing are out of scope for edits. Several confident calls landed on real cards: the dock props in p01, the diploma backdrop in p07, the guitar stage in p12 and the sneaker horizon in p03. The real cards do use invented props and hard floor lines, so I am not asking for those to be removed.** No change. These calls were not used.
