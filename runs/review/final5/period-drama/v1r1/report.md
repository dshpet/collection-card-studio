## Art director

Close, but most generated cards still stand out. The top hat could pass, and the parasol is one fix away. The parasol, fan, cake stand and candelabra share one root cause: photographic micro-detail (punched lace, cloth weave, satin sheen, sharp brass reflections, glowing flame haze) where the real cards use a few broad, smooth shapes. The pale fan and the cake stand also lack value separation from their grounds and a clear side light. The candelabra is also small in an empty frame, which a note can't fix. Several specialists picked real cards as the odd one (booth, sneakers, neckerchief, fan B), and some fixes contradicted the producer's notes; I overruled those. The style text stays unchanged. Four cards get edit notes and the top hat is kept.

1. **Photographic micro-detail that the real cards simplify away: punched-eyelet lace on the parasol and fan, visible weave on the parasol canopy and the tablecloth, satin sheen on every fan pleat, and many small sharp reflections plus hazy glow halos on the candelabra. This is the strongest tell on the sheet. Real cards (chest, booth, fan B, scarf, cooler) turn each material into a few broad, smooth shapes.** Paint lace as a smooth soft cream scalloped band made of a few broad shapes. Paint cloth and paper as smooth matte surfaces. Paint metal with a few broad soft highlights, and flames as clean stylized teardrops.
2. **The hero shares a value with its ground, so it does not stand out. The pale pink fan is almost white on light blue. The cream cake stand melts into a white tablecloth under a near-black wall. Both are lit almost frontally, with weak shadow sides. Real cards set a lighter or darker hero against a soft centered glow and give it one clear side light, with darker undersides and a soft cast shadow.** Light the object from the upper left, so its right side and undersides are clearly darker. Make it read lighter or darker than the background around it, with a soft glow just behind it.
3. **Hard white glossy streaks on dark or metal heroes (top hat crown, candelabra brass) give a CG-render look. It is milder than the issues above, and the top hat stays within reach of the real cards.** Highlights on gloss are few, broad and soft.
4. **Overruled specialist calls. Several picked a real card as the odd one: the fan and booth (colour, lighting, edges), and the sneakers, neckerchief and booth (shape). Some fixes contradict the producer's notes, so I am setting them aside: tilting the parasol 20°, turning the fan to three-quarter or foreshortening it, turning the candelabra to three-quarter, and thinning the top hat's brim. Making the candelabra larger in the frame is a scale change that edit notes cannot ask for, so I am noting it only.** No action. Keep the producer's front views and gentle lean.

- p01: Parasol: Simplify the lace trim into a smooth, soft cream scalloped band with no punched holes. Paint the canopy as smooth, soft matte fabric with no visible weave, with the ribs as slightly softer seams. Keep the parasol, the handle, the knob, the yellow diamond backdrop and the view.
- p03: Cake stand: Paint the tablecloth a soft mid-tone rose with smooth fabric and no weave, clearly darker than the cream stand. Make the purple wall a slightly lighter violet with a soft glow behind the stand. Give the porcelain crisp glossy highlights on the plate rims and the knob, with slightly darker plate undersides and a darker right side on the column. Keep the stand, the cakes, the striped wall and the view.
- p04: keep
- p05: Candelabra: Paint the flames as clean, simple stylized teardrops with only a small, tight warm glow. Paint the brass as smooth warm gold with a few broad, soft highlights in place of the many small sharp reflections. Give the candles a soft warm lit side and a slightly darker right side. Keep the candelabra, the teal backdrop, the floor shadow and the view.
- p06: Folding Fan: Paint the pleats a slightly deeper rose pink, lit from the upper left, with a bright face and a darker face on each pleat and the right half gently darker. Replace the realistic lace with a smooth, soft cream scalloped band made of a few broad shapes. Add a soft shadow of the fan down and to the right on a slightly deeper blue wave backdrop. Keep the fan, the sticks, the gold pin and the view.
