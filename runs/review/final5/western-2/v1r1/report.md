## Art director

Close but not fully there. The specialists who found Campfire A also found real cards on the two control pairs, so their accuracy here is mixed. On A they agree: it is a busy, all-warm scene with props and background clutter, and the fire doesn't light its surroundings. The rendering, finish and palette otherwise sit within the real cards' range. Two earlier edits that removed the props or hazed the background both lost to their drafts. So this note keeps the props and makes three small changes: remove the floating embers, cool the stones slightly, and add firelight to the stones and kettle.

1. **Campfire card reads as a busy scene rather than one item: a kettle, a log cabin, a row of pines, about fifteen scattered pebbles, grass tufts and floating ember crescents all compete with the fire. The background carries as much linework as the hero. Staging, composition, edges and viewpoint all describe this from different sides, and it is the strongest giveaway. Two earlier edits that removed the kettle, cabin and pebbles both lost, so for this redraw leave the props and change only the small clutter.** Remove the floating ember crescents around the flame. Leave the props in place.
2. **The whole campfire card is pulled warm: orange dirt, orange logs, an amber cabin and an orange fire, with olive-brown shadows. Only the pines and kettle are cool. Real cards set the warm hero against a cooler surrounding.** Paint the stones a cool blue-grey, and make the pines a slightly deeper, cooler blue-green so the fire is the main warm mass.
3. **The fire glows but doesn't light anything around it. The stones are lit evenly from above, and the kettle is lit from the front-left, not by the fire.** Add a warm orange glow on the stone faces turned toward the flames and a soft orange rim on the kettle's fire side.
4. **Overruled: on p01 and p02 several specialists picked a real card as the odd one, citing the ship wheel's sunburst and planks, the coaster's saturation and the window's centered framing. Those are real cards in this game, so these traits are part of the house look. They must not become rules.** No change. Real cards may use sunbursts, plank floors, centered frontal objects and full-saturation color.

- p03: Campfire: Remove the small floating orange ember crescents around the flame. Paint the stones of the ring a slightly cooler blue-grey, with a warm orange glow on the faces turned toward the fire, and add a soft orange rim of firelight on the kettle's side facing the flames. Keep the fire, the logs, the stone ring, the kettle, the cabin, the pines, the ground, the pebbles, the grass tufts and the view.
