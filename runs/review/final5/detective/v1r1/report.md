## Art director

Both generated cards come close to the real ones: they share the same clean 3D toy-render look, saturated backdrops and simple staging. Neither reads as clearly foreign, and most specialists picked the real card as the fake. The tell is value. The handcuffs sit as one light-middle grey that barely separates from the pink floor. The magnifier's lens takes the background green, so the glass dissolves into it. Both need only small value and shading edits. Proportion and viewpoint complaints about the magnifier are overruled by the producer's notes.

1. **Handcuffs (p01 B, generated): the whole card sits in one light-middle value. The grey steel and the pink floor differ in hue but barely in lightness. The only darks are thin crescents inside the rings, so at small size the cuffs read as a faint grey squiggle. Value_contrast and composition_hierarchy correctly picked this card. Five other specialists picked the real sneakers, and I overrule them: the hard wall seam, neon trims, mesh and twin-shoe staging are all on a real card.** Handcuffs: deepen the steel to a cool mid-grey, with darker blue-grey on the inner curves and under the lock boxes and one crisp bright highlight along the top of each ring. Keep the finish soft satin. Add a slightly darker, firmer contact shadow under both cuffs and a soft lighter glow on the floor just behind them.
2. **Magnifier (p02 A, generated): the lens glass repeats the background green at the same value, so the glass and the ring field merge. The magnifier also gets little turning shade, so it reads as pasted flat on the rings. Value_contrast saw this. Shape_proportions wanted a long slim handle and viewpoint_depth wanted a 30-degree turn. I overrule both: the producer fixed a straight-on near-circle lens and a short chunky handle. I also don't ask for a bigger crop, because the framing is acceptable.** Magnifier: tint the lens a pale, clear cool blue-grey that is lighter than the green behind it, with one soft curved reflection on its upper left. Make the right side of the metal ring and the underside of the red handle slightly darker. Add a soft, faint shadow of the magnifier on the rings.
3. **Both generated cards use a flat, even backdrop with no lift behind the hero. The real hoop card has a lighter halo behind the object that helps it pop. This is a small shared point, and it is already folded into both notes.** Add a soft, slightly lighter glow behind the hero. Keep it subtle and keep the existing backdrop pattern.

- p01: Handcuffs: Paint the steel a slightly deeper cool grey in soft satin, with one crisp bright highlight along the top of each ring and darker cool blue-grey on the inner curves and under the lock boxes, so the cuffs read clearly darker than the floor. Add a slightly darker, firmer contact shadow under both cuffs and a soft lighter glow on the coral floor just behind them. Keep the cuffs, the chain, the locks, the keyholes, the pink floor and the view.
- p02: Magnifier: Tint the lens glass a pale, clear cool blue-grey in place of the background green, with one soft curved reflection along its upper left. Make the right side of the metal ring and the underside of the red handle slightly darker, and add a soft faint shadow of the magnifier on the rings. Keep the magnifier, the green ring backdrop and the view.
