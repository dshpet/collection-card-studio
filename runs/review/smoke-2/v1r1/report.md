## Art director

Close. The cowboy hat could pass as real. The lasso pig nearly does: the only tells are the pebbly ground and a pale, evenly lit value range. The saddle is the weakest card, given away by dashed stitching, chrome stirrups, flat lighting and the extra lower rail. The specialists were unreliable: they picked the real card as fake on p02 almost unanimously, and they flagged the controls too. Several of their patterns (mute colors, strip settings, crop the hero) describe real-card traits and are overruled.

1. **Added fine surface marks that real cards smooth away. The clearest give-away is the saddle's dashed contrast stitching along every edge, together with crisp buckles and chrome stirrups. The pig card has the same problem in its scattered pebbles and dust specks. Real cards put marks on objects only as a few big, soft lines.** Draw seams as a few soft solid lines. Draw metal as soft satin with one broad highlight. Keep the ground as smooth, even areas with no scattered specks.
2. **The light is flat and frontal, and small parts drop almost no contact shadow. On the saddle, the seat and flap are lit evenly and the stirrups cast nothing. On the pig, the right flank barely turns and the hooves don't darken the dirt. Real cards (the train pig, the gloves) have one clear upper-left light with a darker side. Lighting picked both of these correctly.** Light from the upper left. Give the right side of the hero a clearly darker shade, and add a soft contact shadow wherever it touches its support.
3. **On the lasso-pig card, the hero is not set apart by value. The pig, the fence and the dirt all sit in one pale mid band, and the pig separates only by hue. The real train pig uses big light and dark groups. Value and composition caught this card; the other specialists overlooked it.** Make the background a gentle step darker than the hero so the hero is the lightest big shape.
4. **Overruled. Many specialists called real cards fake because of traits the real set really has. These include the jacket's badges, piping and bright orange cuffs, the train's saturated red and green with a busy hill and track scene, the bottles' tent setting, the station's inked clock, and the umbrella's puffy scalloped canopy. The real cards are rich, saturated and often full scenes, and centered floating heroes are fine. Do not strip settings, mute colors or flatten detail across the board. The cowboy hat, which every specialist passed as real, shows the target look.** Keep current saturation, scene richness and framing; change only the items above.

- p01: Pig Roper: Remove the scattered pebbles and dust specks and make the dirt a smooth, even warm tone. Make the fence and ground slightly darker so the pig is the lightest shape. Light from the upper left so the pig's right flank is slightly darker, and add a soft shadow under each hoof.
- p02: keep
- p04: Saddle: Replace the dashed stitching with a few soft solid seam lines, and make the stirrups and buckles soft satin grey. Remove the lower fence rail and keep the blue stripes. Keep the saddle's shape and color.
