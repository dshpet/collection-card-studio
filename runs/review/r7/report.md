## Art director

Close, but not there yet. The whistle could pass and is kept. The taxi and tea cup are near and need small edits. The bugle, barrel and pot still show up on sight through swollen shapes, added parts and a higher camera. The basket and tent mainly need added or oversized parts removed.

The rendering, palette and finish are in the right family, so the style text now targets camera, part count and side light, and keeps its one-line ask for slim parts. Scale stays at four fifths, because the real bugle is slim and asking for more size would swell it further.

shape_proportions was the most reliable report, and viewpoint_depth was right on the cards where it counts most. Heavy lighting, value and edge pushes are left out so this round does not swing the other way.

1. **Parts swell and extras get added. Bells, pots, rims, tassels, tent stakes and bumpers grow fat, and the generator adds rivets, leaves, rope lashings, ring lugs, stone rings and foot rings. This is the strongest tell, visible on p02, p04, p05, p06, p07 and p08. The real cards have fewer parts and slimmer attachments.** Draw only the parts the subject names, at true size. Use slim rims, cords, bands and posts, plain metal hoops, plain posts and apples with short stems.
2. **The camera is too high and too far back. Openings gape on the barrel and tea cup, more ground shows, and whole scenes are fitted neatly inside the card. The real barrel, cup, basket and pot sit low and close, with thin rim ovals, and the real fire crops at the bottom edge.** Use a low, close camera just above the object's top, so openings are thin ovals and little ground shows. Scenes may crop at the bottom edge.
3. **Light is too even and frontal. Form stays one mid tone and darkness sits in a separate cast-shadow blob, most clearly on the barrel, pot and bugle. The real barrel and bugle turn slowly from a lit side into one broad shaded side.** One soft side light turns slowly into a broad shaded side. The shadow on the ground stays soft and close under the object.
4. **Metal and glazed surfaces are over-managed: hard mirror bands on the bugle, even lacquer on the basket apples, and raised sticker decoration on the cup flower. The real metal has broad, calm highlights.** Shiny materials carry one broad, soft highlight. Painted decoration lies flat in the surface.
5. **Backgrounds differ on single cards: neon cyan bloom on the bugle, and a hard wall-and-floor split on the tea cup. Both are specific to those cards, because the real basket has a clean table line and the real barrel has a bright cyan sky, so this is not a style rule.** Handle in the p02 and p03 redraw notes only.

Style text:

The attached images are real cards from the game. Draw one new card that could sit beside them unnoticed, matching their rendering, lighting, finish, color and detail.
Use a low, close camera just above the object's top: openings are thin ovals, little ground shows. It spans about four fifths of the card; scenes may crop at the bottom.
True proportions, slim rims, cords, bands and posts, and only the parts the subject names.
One soft side light turns slowly into a broad shaded side, with a soft shadow close under it.
Shiny materials carry one broad highlight; cloth, cord and wood stay matte.
No text, letters, numbers, frame, border or watermark.

- p01: keep
- p02: Bugle (B). Make the bell clearly smaller against the tube. Make the rope a thin, narrow wrap with two small tassels on thin cords. Use flat, even teal rays with a plain center.
- p03: Tea Cup (B). Lower the camera slightly so only a thin sliver of tea shows and the cup sits low on its saucer. Use one continuous red background that fades softly into the surface. Paint the flower flat under the glaze.
- p04: Basket (B). Use four large apples with short stems and no leaves. Lower the camera to rim level and move closer, so only a narrow strip of table shows. Make the table shadow pale and soft.
- p05: Cargo Barrel (B). Lower the camera so the top is a thin flat oval of rim. Use plain steel hoops grouped in pairs near each end. Light from the right, so the left side falls into soft deep shadow.
- p06: Taxi (A). Lengthen the body slightly. Make the wheels slightly smaller, set into the arches, and slim the chrome bumper. Make the road smooth and plain.
- p07: Tent (A). Shrink the stakes to small pegs on thin guy lines. Make the canvas a slightly dusty ochre with no orange trim. Remove the yellow flowers.
- p08: Campfire Cook (B). Draw a wide, shallow pot with a thin flat rim and a slim wire handle, and no ring lugs. Use plain forked posts with no lashings or hanging loop. Move closer so the bottom edge crops the fire, with no stones.
