## Art director

Yes, this is close. The objects, rendering and most backdrops already belong with the real cards, and the rocket and meteor could pass as they are, so both are keeps. The remaining tells are mostly staging, not the hero objects: the grey or lilac clay ground on the telescope, rover and dome, the grey floor on the console, and the tight white halo on the planet. Next come the pink-cast helmet and the polished finish on the saucer, console and dish base. The other eight cards get small colour, light and finish notes.

Overruled:
- All form findings and framing or camera requests are dropped as out of scope for this round.
- value_contrast's p09, p10 and p11 entries describe the cards of p11, p09 and p10, so I used them by content, not by number.
- Calls that picked a real card over the generated one are overruled: lighting p05 (sneakers), surface and edges p07 (pool bar), value p12 (submarine) and shape p12 (submarine).
- Findings on the controls are false alarms: jacket and chair in staging, jacket in shape and surface, book in surface. Confidence 3 alone is therefore not reliable.
- Several claimed formula tells also appear on real cards, so they are not tells: thin dense rays and a dark vignette (jacket), a curved table edge and soft floor reflections (chair), paired clouds and small pebbles (sandcastle), rivets and lacquer (submarine), and a broad central glow (submarine). The console's reflections are replaced only because the grey floor beneath them goes.
- The helmet's "patchy halo" is not visible on the card.
- Pinprick stars stay on the dome.

1. **The ground is a generic grey or lilac clay surface. It has no local colour and no material, and it shares nothing with the backdrop. This is the strongest tell and it repeats on p01, p04 and p07. p11 has the same fault as a flat grey floor.** Give the ground one real surface in a colour related to the scene, such as warm wooden boards, warm dusty tan or sandy peach. Draw a soft shadow in a darker shade of that ground colour, darkest where the object touches.
2. **A tight white glow hugs the hero's edge on p08. It washes out the backdrop around the planet and leaves the darkest tone in the background. The real glows on the submarine and jacket are wide and fade slowly into the field.** Run the backdrop pattern right up to the hero's edge. If a glow is wanted, make it wide and faint, centred behind the object and fading slowly into the field.
3. **Neutrals and shadows lose their own colour. The helmet's white goes pink, the dome trim goes cream, the rover's orange falls to dull brick, and the planet's dark side stays the same orange.** Keep whites clean with cool blue-grey shadows. Let coloured parts stay saturated in shadow and step to a deeper related hue, such as orange to red-brown.
4. **Finish is over-polished on the machines. The saucer has wide brushed-metal streaks, the console is one hard polished plastic, and the dish base is wet lacquer. Rivets and glossy metal alone are not the fault, because the real submarine has both.** Use smooth satin paint or metal with one broad soft sheen. Keep the small sharp highlights to one or two spots.
5. **Value jumps too hard inside the hero. Near-white sits directly against near-black on the dish base and the saucer hull, with thin middle tones.** Move the darkest areas up to deep mid tones, with a gradual turn from light to shade. Keep near-black to small contact shadows.
6. **Some plain backdrops are slightly too clean, like smooth digital gradients. Their measured detail falls below the real floor on p05, p08, p10 and p11. This is a minor issue.** Slightly strengthen the steps in the backdrop pattern and add gentle hand-painted tonal variation.

- p01: Replace the grey mound with warm wooden decking with wide boards, filling the ground to the card edges. Add a soft dark contact shadow under the three tripod feet, in a deeper brown of the boards.
- p02: keep
- p03: Slightly lighten the blue base to a satin mid blue with one broad soft highlight. Add a gradual light-to-shade turn across the inside of the dish. Slightly soften the shadow on the sand so it fades with distance.
- p04: Change the grey ground to warm dusty tan. Make the shadow under the rover a soft dark warm brown, darkest under the tyres. Keep the orange body rich and saturated into its shadows.
- p05: Make the helmet shell clean neutral white with cool blue-grey shadows. Change the floor to a calm medium lavender, clearly darker than the shell. Add a soft dark contact shadow under the base.
- p07: Change the lavender ground to warm dusty tan with a few shallow craters. Make the dome trim neutral white with soft blue-grey shadows. Let warm yellow door light spill onto the steps and the ground in front.
- p08: Let the purple stripes run right up to the planet, with only a wide faint brightening in the centre of the card. Turn the planet's shadow side to deep red-brown with a hint of purple. Make the highlight on the ring a broad soft sheen.
- p10: Paint the saucer hull as smooth silver with one broad soft highlight band. Slightly darken the upper hull to a soft mid silver so its edge stands out from the yellow, and lift the darkest underside to dark grey. Slightly strengthen the steps between the yellow rings.
- p11: Change the grey floor to a matte warm sandy peach that picks up orange bounce from the wall, with one soft shadow under the console in place of the coloured reflections. Make the teal body satin painted metal. Make the buttons soft matte plastic with one small highlight each.
- p12: keep
