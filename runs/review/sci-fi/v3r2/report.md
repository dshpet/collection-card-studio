## Art director

Yes, this is close. The objects, palette energy and rendering sit in the real family, and the p04 parchment and p07 observatory could pass unchanged. p09, p11 and p01 need only light edits. The remaining gap is finish rather than form: flat or hard backdrops with no glow behind the hero, photo-polished metal on p05, p06 and p12, and large near-black or hard-white value extremes. Specialist confidence is modest. Five specialists flagged a card in control pair p08, and they split on p04. The style text stays settled, and the fixes are small per-card edits.

1. **Backdrops are flat or hard-patterned, with no bright glow behind the hero. The real cards put a soft bright pool of light behind the object and let the corners darken gently (p08 B, p12 B, p10 B, p01 B). The generated cards show a dead-flat magenta wall (p06), dark waves with no centre (p10), a hard two-tone diamond pattern (p09), many narrow hard rays (p01) and a star field with an added planet (p03). This is the most visible tell across the set.** Behind the object, paint a soft bright glow that fades outward to slightly darker corners. Keep any pattern wide, soft and tone-on-tone, one close step lighter or darker than the field.
2. **Metal looks like a product photo or polished chrome: brushed streaks, long sharp reflections, tiny crisp rivets and hairline seams, all equally sharp (p06 tube, p05 rocket, p12 capsule). Real cards paint metal simply, as on the keyboard stand and the backpack buckles. Gray metal also drifts toward steel blue on p12. Surface, lighting and palette specialists all describe this one fault.** Paint metal as soft, slightly dull painted metal in neutral grey, with one broad gentle highlight and smooth shading. Use a few soft rivets and thin quiet seams.
3. **Value extremes replace the middle tones. A large solid near-black sits on the capsule underside (p12), a very dark lower half on the globe (p09), and sharp white glints on the comet facets (p10). Real cards keep black and white to small accents and let shadow slide through deep color.** Paint large shadow areas in deep rich color with soft reflected light near the edge. Use a broad soft sheen for highlights, and keep pure black and white to tiny accents.
4. **Cool heroes sit on cool grounds with no warm mass, so the card has one color family. The station is blue on blue (p03) and the comet is cool blue on violet (p10). Real cards set a warm hero on a cool ground or the reverse (bike, scarf, keyboard).** Give the hero one clear warm or cream color area that contrasts with the ground, while keeping the background vivid.
5. **Lit objects and practicals do not affect what is around them. The plant bulbs light nothing, and the cast shadows on p05 and p11 are harder and darker than the soft ones on real cards.** Let glowing parts tint nearby surfaces slightly. Draw cast shadows soft and tinted with the surface color.
6. **Overruled. All shape, proportion, thickness and framing-size fixes are dropped because form cannot be judged across different objects. Viewpoint re-posing is dropped for the same reason. Edges_linework is also set aside: it picked the real card in p01, p02, p05, p06, p10 and p11, and heavy closed contours appear on real cards too. Advice to mute colors (p07, p10, p11) is rejected because real cards are this saturated. On p09 the stars and ring stay, since they are part of the subject. On p06 the straw stays as an in-theme extra. Five specialists flagged a card in control pair p08, and edges flagged p02, so out-of-range numbers alone carry no weight here.** No change.

- p01: Use about half as many sunburst rays, wider and softer, with only a small step between light and dark rays. Add a soft bright glow behind the rock.
- p03: Replace the Earth and stars with a plain blue background that has a soft bright glow behind the station and slightly darker corners. Paint the hull warm cream with a soft cool shadow side. Make the white rims on the solar panels slightly softer.
- p04: keep
- p05: Paint the rocket body soft warm white with one broad gentle highlight and fewer, softer rivets. Make the sky a slightly cleaner blue, lighter near the horizon. Soften the tower's shadow on the pad into a gentle pool.
- p06: Paint the tube as simple soft metal with one broad highlight and smooth dents in place of the brushed streaks. Give the magenta wall a soft bright glow behind the tube and slightly darker corners. Add a soft shadow on the shelf beside the tube.
- p07: keep
- p09: Lighten the globe's shadow side to deep blue with a soft transition. Make the diamonds slightly closer in tone and add a soft bright glow behind the globe. Make the orange metal slightly more satin.
- p10: Brighten the violet field, make the waves only slightly darker than it, and add a soft bright glow behind the comet with slightly darker corners. Replace the sharp white glints with a broad soft sheen and softly blended facets. Add a warm pale-yellow glow at the comet's head.
- p11: Make the leaves and stems slightly more matte, leaving only the bulbs glassy. Soften the pot's cast shadow and lighten it slightly. Let the bulbs spread a soft yellow tint onto nearby leaves.
- p12: Paint the underside deep mid blue with soft bounced yellow light along the rim. Paint the capsule in soft neutral grey with one broad highlight. Use fewer, softer rivets and a quiet seam.
