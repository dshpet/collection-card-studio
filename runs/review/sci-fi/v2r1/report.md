## Art director

Yes, this is close. Most of the set could sit beside the real cards, and most specialist calls were low confidence. Finish, palette and background type are right: rays, rings, waves and diamonds all appear on real cards. The p11 flask is kept. p01, p02, p03 and p08 need only light edits. The clear giveaways are p06 (icon wall, tile floor, crowded panel) and p12 (neon lab, one teal hue, flat grey value). p07 and p09 are given away by the tile floor, and p10 by rivets.

Overruled: edges_linework picked the real card in 8 of 10 test pairs and flagged the control cards, so its contour findings are dropped. The controls show that hard rays (p04), diamond walls (p05 A) and busy blurred backgrounds (p05 B) are real, so harlequin walls and sunbursts are not tells. Staging's low-detail range is unreliable for the same reason. Color_palette was wrong on p08, p10, p11 and the p05 control, and its calls for muted backgrounds and cool grey-white are overruled; the real p06 monitor is also ivory, and its blue side plane is the real difference. Lighting_shading was wrong on p11 and flagged the p04 control.

All shape_proportions fixes are out of scope, and camera, crop and framing fixes from viewpoint_depth and composition_hierarchy are not in the notes. That leaves one remaining difference the notes do not touch: generated heroes are still more whole and centred than real ones.

1. **Stock staging: white or tinted tile floors with grout lines and a coloured sheen, plus invented wall motifs and glowing trim. No real card on these sheets has any of this. It is the strongest tell on p06, p07, p09 and p12.** Stand the object on one simple surface: wide wooden boards, plain concrete or a plain coloured floor, with one soft shadow. Keep the wall a plain gradient or a wide tone-on-tone pattern.
2. **Crowded fine detail: screws, LED rows, vent slats, rivets, bolts, panel lines and seat ribbing, all equally crisp. Real cards keep a few large soft parts and little small detail. This is clearest on p06 and p10, milder on p07 and p08.** Use a few large, smooth, softly painted parts with one or two small details at the focus. Keep metal and plastic surfaces clean.
3. **Even, frontal light with a shallow shadow side. The hero sits at the same grey as its background and separates only by hue. Real heroes have one large dark shape and a cool shadow side with a clear corner. This shows on p01, p03, p06, p09 and p12.** Light from the upper left. Let the far side of the object fall into one broad, cool, darker shadow, with a clear change at the corner of the form.
4. **One hue spreads across the hero and the setting, so metal and walls lose their own colour. p12 is all teal and p03 is mostly blue on peach. Loud backgrounds are not the fault: real cards use loud magenta, cyan and yellow, so the suggested dusty or muted backgrounds are overruled.** Keep metal neutral grey with a warm highlight, and keep the object's main colour different from the room.
5. **A soft bloom hugs the hero on p01, p02, p08 and p10. Real backgrounds do brighten broadly at the centre, so keep that. Only the tight glow around the object's edge is wrong.** Let the background brighten broadly behind the object, with its pattern running evenly up to the object's edge.
6. **Streaky brushed marks and an identical sheen on every part. The metal on p02 is noisy, and p12's cap and base share one grey shine with its hazy glass. Real cards keep matte and glossy parts distinct.** Paint metal smooth with one clean soft highlight, and keep rubber, cloth and plastic matte.

- p01: Slightly darken the lower right of the blue sphere into a deep navy shadow. Remove the pale glow hugging the sphere and run the pink rings evenly up to its edge.
- p02: Paint the tanks as smooth steel with one clean soft highlight on each left side, and let their right sides go slightly darker and cool. Remove the pink bloom around the tanks.
- p03: Let the right third of the dome and wall fall into a slightly darker cool blue-grey shadow. Make the lit walls a slightly more neutral off-white, with a small dark accent under the blue roof band.
- p06: Replace the icon wall and tile floor with a plain, richer blue gradient and a simple wooden desk with a soft shadow. Make the right side panel a deep cool blue-grey with a clear corner. Remove the screws, most of the LEDs and the lower vent slats, leaving the two reels as the focus.
- p07: Replace the tile floor with one plain light concrete surface, with no grout lines or blue reflection. Make the cast shadow soft and darkest near the base. Remove the small bolts.
- p08: Remove the pale glow behind the car. Slightly deepen the underside into a broad darker teal. Make the seats plain matte, with no ribbing.
- p09: Replace the tile floor with wide wooden table boards and a soft warm shadow, with no coloured reflections. Let the right side of the phone fall into a deeper cool green.
- p10: Remove most of the rivets and the double bevel lines so the body is one smooth painted form. Make the gold slightly satin, and slightly darken the lower right of the body. Keep the diamond background.

- p12: Replace the neon arch, glowing bar and tile seams with a plain, slightly deeper blue-teal wall of a few large panels and one quiet floor. Make the cap and base neutral grey with a warm highlight and one darker side. Give the glass one thin sharp highlight and slightly less haze.
