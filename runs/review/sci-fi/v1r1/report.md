## Art director

Close, but not yet convincing as a set. Modeling, charm and backdrops are on target. The rocket and meteor could pass as they are. The helmet, saucer, planet and dish need only small edits. The console, moon base, telescope and rover still stand out, mainly because of grainy dressed ground, colorless bodies or both.

Overruled:
- All shape, thickness and proportion fixes, plus viewpoint and framing notes, because they are out of scope for edits or depend on comparing different objects.
- Every call that named a real card as the odd one, including the sneaker, audio player and scarf lighting, the column's value and color, and the taxi and pool bar edges.
- The moon-soil-under-stripes complaint, because real cards also put flat backdrops over ground. Only the speckle and filler are wrong.
- The planet's inked contour and the telescope's halo, which are barely visible on the cards.
- The rule that frontal light marks every generated card; it holds only for the console, moon base and dish.

Caution: several weak picks lean on the sci-fi subject rather than on visible evidence, so the rocket and meteor are kept. The bold paint is a color change only and should stay moderate, because white and cream heroes do exist on real cards.

1. **Grainy, dressed ground: the three moon cards share speckled regolith sprinkled with pebbles and small craters, and the console stands on stamped tread plate. Real grounds are a few big, clean shapes such as road, planks, cobbles or plain ledges. This is the strongest shared giveaway.** Moon Base, Telescope, Rover: draw the ground as smooth painted grey with one or two large soft mounds and a soft shadow under the object. Console: draw a plain smooth metal floor. Keep the stripes and sunbursts behind them, because flat backdrops over a ground plane are normal on real cards.
2. **Colorless hero on a loud backdrop: the rover, console, dish and telescope are white, grey or cream, so the background carries all the color. Real heroes hold their own local color. Silver parts are fine as an accent or on a hero with strong color elsewhere, as on the rocket.** Rover, Console, Dish, Telescope: paint the main body in a bold enamel color and keep white, grey or brass as trim. The hero should hold more color than its backdrop.
3. **One finish across the whole object: painted panels, bare metal and seals all collapse into the same soft satin, with seams moulded in softly. This merges the surface reports with the doubled-rim and closed-contour complaints.** Console, Dish, Rover: use a duller painted body, thin crisp seam lines, small screws and bright bare-metal trim. Keep the strongest gloss on buttons, lamps or glass.
4. **Soft frontal light on large simple forms: the panel face, moon dome and dish bowl stay in one bright value with a shallow far side. Real cards commit to one side light with a broad shadow side. The rule applies to these three, not to all generated cards.** Console, Moon Base, Dish: light from the upper left and let the right side fall into one broad cool shadow.
5. **Dull or single-tinted shadows and neutrals: the moon base scene is washed in lilac, and the saucer underside turns grey-brown. Real shadows stay colored and each material keeps its own color.** Tint shadows blue-violet and keep them rich. Moon Base: use purple only in the sky, and give the ground and white frame their own neutral colors.
6. **Backdrops a little too even: the planet's stripes lack the center glow that the real stripe cards have, and the helmet's floor is blank white with no tie to the wall.** Planet: add a soft bright glow behind the hero with slightly darker corners. Helmet: tint the floor toward the wall color with a gentle tone change.

- p01: Console: paint the body deep teal enamel, with grey only on the trim and bezels. Replace the tread plate with a plain smooth metal floor and a soft shadow under the console. Light from the upper left so the right end and lower edge fall into one cool shadow.
- p02: Helmet: tint the floor slightly pink with a gentle tone change toward the back. Shade the lower shell one step darker than the floor. Add a few fine seam lines on the shell.
- p03: Saucer: make the hull clean brushed silver with fine streaks following the curve and one soft highlight band. Tint the underside shadow rich blue-violet.
- p04: keep
- p05: Moon Base: make the ground smooth neutral grey with soft warm and cool variation, keeping purple only in the sky. Replace the large crater and small craters with one or two soft low mounds. Light from the upper left, with a cool shadow on the right of the dome and tunnel.
- p06: Planet: add a soft bright glow behind the planet and let the stripes darken slightly toward the corners. Mute the purple slightly so it sits behind the hero. Give the ring a smooth, slightly glossy finish with a thin highlight.
- p08: keep
- p09: Dish: paint the bowl white enamel with a red band and the pylon deep blue enamel, with thin seams and small bolts. Grade the inside of the bowl down to a cool mid shadow on one side.
- p11: Telescope: paint the tube deep navy enamel, keeping the brass bands. Draw the ground as smooth painted grey with one or two soft mounds and a single soft shadow. Use finer, quieter wood grain on the legs with a slightly duller oiled finish.
- p12: Rover: paint the body red-orange enamel with white panels as trim and thin crisp seams. Draw the ground as smooth painted grey with one or two large soft mounds. Tint the shadow under the rover blue-violet.
