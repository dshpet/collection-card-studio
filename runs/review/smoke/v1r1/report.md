## Art director

Close. The hat passes as a real card, and the specialists mostly picked the real jacket over it. The saddle gives itself away mainly through dashed stitching on every edge, plus the missing shadow on the rail. The pig gives itself away through a card-wide orange cast and flat, even light on its body. Both fixes are small. Specialists called both controls (suitcase, bus) generated with confidence, so their 'too detailed / too outlined' patterns overstate the case. Real cards carry crisp badges, stickers and scuffs, so don't push toward softness. The previous saddle edit lost with four changes, including grain removal and pad recolour. This note is cut down to two changes.

1. **Dashed stitch lines along every leather edge. On the saddle, an even pale dotted seam traces the seat rim, fender, skirt and strap, all at the same weight. This was also the tell on the generated gloves in the lessons, and the real cards never show it. Specialists who flagged general over-detail are overruled: the real jacket (badges, pocket flaps, snaps) and the real suitcase (scuffs, die-cut stickers, dark contour) carry plenty of crisp detail. So the tell is the repeated dotted seam, not detail in general.** Draw seams as a few soft solid lines at the main panel edges only. Keep badges, buckles, stickers and hardware crisp as they are.
2. **The whole card is pushed to one warm orange-tan family with no cool or neutral area to rest the eye. On the pig scene, the ground, mesas, fence, hat and pig all sit in one orange-peach range, and only a thin strip of sky is cool. Real scenes (the pig at the car, the bus) keep grey road, green foliage and blue sky around the warm hero. Color_palette's p01/p04 calls are overruled: both of those pairs are real, and their purple and lime backgrounds are part of the real look.** Surround a warm hero with some true neutrals and cool tones: grey-beige ground, grey-brown weathered wood, green plants, blue sky.
3. **Light comes softly from the front, and the hero drops little or no shadow onto what it rests on. The saddle sits on the rail with no cast shadow. The pig's body is an even peach with only tiny shadows under the hooves. Real cards (gloves, bus, hat, car scene) commit to one side light, with a darker far side and a clear shadow on the support or ground. The call on the real jacket is overruled: its shading is soft but acceptable, so ask for this only moderately.** Light from the upper left. Make the far side and undersides slightly darker, and cast a soft, clear shadow onto the surface the object touches.
4. **A faint pale glow behind the hero in radial backgrounds. The hat's cyan rays lighten slightly toward the centre. The effect is minor and edits that only calmed a glow have lost before (Cactus, Horseshoe), so treat this as low priority. Calls on p01 B and p04 B are overruled, since those are real cards with the same mild centre glow.** Keep sunburst rays running in to the centre at an even value.
5. **Overruled, not actionable: the proportion, inflation and corner-rate calls (pillowy suitcase, squat bus, merged gloves) all fall on real cards. Form can't be compared across different objects. The viewpoint and composition calls on the real suitcase and saddle framing are also overruled, because the real suitcase is just as centred and front-on.** No change to form, proportions, camera or framing.

- p02: keep
- p03: Saddle: Replace the dashed stitching with a few soft solid seam lines at the main panel edges. Cast a soft dark shadow from the saddle onto the wooden rail just below and to its right. Keep the rest of the card as is.
- p05: Pig Roper: Make the dirt ground a paler dusty grey-beige with fewer pebbles, and the fence weathered grey-brown wood. Light the pig from the upper left so its right side and belly underside are slightly darker. Keep the pig, hat, lasso and sky as they are.
