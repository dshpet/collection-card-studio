## Art director

Close, but not unnoticed: rendering and overall color family match, yet every generated card stands out as larger, fatter and more toy-like than its real partner.

The main fault comes from the current style text. 'Nearly touching the card edges' and 'few big smooth forms' drive the two biggest tells, overfill and inflation, so I replaced both lines. 'A few bold marks instead of fine grain' made detail too coarse, so that is replaced too.

Trusted: composition_hierarchy (correct on all 8) and shape_proportions (7 of 8). Trusted in part: surface_finish, on p02 to p05 only, and viewpoint_depth, on p02, p05, p06 and p08.

Overruled: lighting_shading, value_contrast, edges_linework and staging_detail, along with the matching notes from color_palette and surface_finish. Most of their complaints describe the real cards: the whistle halo, the tea cup vignette, the glossy basket table, the busy forest, the campfire stone-free ground and pot speckle. Asking for those to be removed would pull the generator away from the game. Their pure, clean simplification fixes are also rejected, because the real cards are more detailed than the generated ones here.

No card is kept; all 8 need the framing and proportion fix.

1. **Overfill. The hero is blown up until it presses on the card edges, leaving little of the scene around it. All 8 generated cards do this, and the current line 'nearly touching the card edges' causes it. The real cards all leave more air.** Replace the framing line. The object fills about three quarters of the card, with air on every side.
2. **Inflation. Thin parts swell into sausages and bodies shorten into chubby toys: bugle tube and tassels, cup handle and rim, basket handle and strips, barrel hoops, taxi bumper and wheels, tent stakes, campfire posts and pot. 'Few big smooth forms' overrides 'thin parts stay slim'.** Drop 'simplified into big smooth forms'. Name the thin parts directly: handles, rims, cords, bands, posts and bumpers stay slim.
3. **One vinyl finish with coarse detail. Porcelain, wicker, wood and rope all read as the same smooth lacquered plastic, with oversized marks such as the sticker flower, wide weave straps and big diagonal barrel grain. The real cards carry finer, quieter detail and more varied surfaces. 'A few bold marks instead of fine grain' pushes this the wrong way.** Ask for fine grain that follows the wood, narrow woven strips and thin painted decoration. Keep one soft highlight on shiny parts only.
4. **Camera too high or too forced. The barrel shows a wide open top, the campfire is seen from above with a splayed fire ring, the bugle shows both tube ends open, and the taxi has a swollen wide-angle front.** Add a low, steady camera to the style text, and fix the mixed views in the card notes.
5. **Background as loud as the hero. Generated fields run deep and fully saturated (gold, electric cyan, vivid red-orange, strong violet, acid grass), so hero and field compete. The real fields are softer or duller in color and let the object lead.** Add one short line: background colors stay softer than the object.

Style text:

The attached images are real cards from the game. Draw one new card that could sit beside them unnoticed: same rendering, lighting, finish, color and detail.
Draw the object with its real proportions from a low, steady camera. Handles, rims, cords, bands, posts and bumpers stay slim.
It fills about three quarters of the card, with air on every side.
One light from one side turns softly into a deep shadow side.
Fine grain follows wood, weaves use narrow strips, painted decoration stays thin; shiny parts carry one soft highlight.
Background colors stay softer than the object.
Draw only what the subject names. No text, frame or watermark.

- p01: Draw the whistle about 20 percent smaller, centered, with the mouth and ring well inside the card. Use a slim steel ring. Make the background a pale, soft yellow with faint campfire motifs.
- p02: Draw a slim brass bugle about 20 percent smaller, with an even narrow tube, a modest bell and a double loop. Show the mouthpiece from the side. Wrap thin braided cord tightly around the tube, ending in two small fringed tassels. Use soft turquoise rays.
- p03: Draw the cup and saucer about 20 percent smaller, sitting low with steam rising through open space. Use warm white porcelain, a thin blue rim line, a slim handle, a small foot and a shallow saucer. Paint a small flower sprig flat in the glaze. Make the background a duller deep red.
- p04: Draw the basket smaller, with air above the handle and table on both sides. Use a thin twisted handle, narrow woven strips and a slim plaited rim. Give the apples faint streaks and soft highlights. Make the wall a paler lilac.
- p05: Draw the barrel about 25 percent smaller, slimmer, with sky above it and deck on both sides. Lower the camera so the top is a thin oval. Use narrow hoops lying flat, a thin rim and fine vertical grain with small nicks along each stave.
- p06: Draw a real sedan taxi in a three-quarter view from further back, smaller on the road. Give it a long body with a separate hood and trunk, normal wheels, a thin chrome bumper, small mirrors, a small roof sign and a fine grille. Show a seat through the windshield.
- p07: Draw the tent smaller and further back, with flat canvas slopes and a straight ridge. Use thin guy lines and small pegs. Mute the canvas to natural ochre with a faint cloth texture. Use deeper, duller forest greens around it, with more trees.
- p08: Draw the frame and pot smaller, with thin forked posts, a slim beam resting in the forks and a thin wire handle. Lower the camera. Use a shallow pot with a thin rim and sooty iron, and a clear gap of air above a small fire of logs cropped by the bottom edge, with no stone ring. Mute the dirt to grey-tan.
