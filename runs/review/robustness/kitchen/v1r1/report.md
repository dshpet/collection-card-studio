## Art director

Close but not there yet. Three of the ten generated cards could pass as they are: the frying pan, the cutting board and the soup pot. The oven mitts need only a small stitching fix. The rest are given away mainly by product-photo staging: studio gradient sweeps, photoreal marble or tile, and brushed or mirror metal with sharp white glints. That shows up on the toaster, kettle, cookie jar, rolling pin and faucet, and the whisk has a murky blurred backdrop. These are background and finish problems, not form problems. The fixes swap in painted sunbursts or smooth painted wood and satin sheens, and keep the objects themselves. The control cards (balloons, signpost butterfly, skyline) show that rainbow color, small props and some detailed backgrounds are within the real style, so the specialist calls against those were overruled.

1. **Product-photo staging. Five generated cards sit on a seamless studio gradient or a photoreal counter (white veined marble, glossy white tile, a pink or purple sweep with a soft floor). That is the clearest sign they are not from this game. On the real cards the background is a painted radial sunburst or a simple stylized painted scene. Affects the toaster, kettle, cookie jar, rolling pin and faucet.** Put the object on a painted radial sunburst of two close tones, or on a smooth painted wooden surface in front of a soft single-color backdrop. Do not use marble, studio sweeps or photo tile.
2. **Photoreal material finish: brushed metal, mirror chrome, lacquered red enamel with pinpoint white glints, and a ceramic glaze that reads like a product render. The real cards use soft satin sheens with one broad highlight and a related, cooler shadow side. Several specialists describe this one problem from the lighting, value, finish and edges sides.** Paint metal and glaze as smooth satin with one broad soft highlight on the upper left and a slightly deeper, cooler shadow side on the right.
3. **The hero shares its value band with a pale floor or wall (cream jar on white marble, silver tap on white tile, tan pin on white marble), so it separates from the background by hue only. On real cards the hero is clearly lighter or darker than the background behind it.** Make the surface under the object a mid tone, slightly darker than the object, so the object reads as the lightest shape.
4. **Murky, undesigned background on the whisk: a blurry green field with hazy light shafts and dark smudged corners. The real flat backgrounds are clean, even sunbursts.** Use an even radial sunburst of light and mid rays with no light shafts or dark corners.
5. **Added stitch detail: dashed stitching and fuzzy felt texture on the oven mitts, the same tell seen on the generated glove in earlier rounds. The real cards keep fabric smooth, with a few soft seams.** Draw fabric smooth, with a few soft solid seam lines.
6. **Overruled claims. The real control cards include a rainbow balloon cluster, a butterfly on the signpost, a windowed city skyline and a busy brick gate. So rainbow hue counts, small whimsical props and some detailed backgrounds are within the real style and should not be corrected. The proportion and roundness notes, the 'too centered or too small' notes and the claim that the pan has a heavy outline are also set aside: form and scale cannot be judged across different objects, and the pan reads like a real card.** No change.

- p01: keep
- p02: Toaster: Replace the pink studio gradient with a pink radial sunburst of light and mid pink rays, and keep a short soft shadow under the feet. Make the body smooth satin chrome without brushed streaks, with a faint cool blue tint on its shadow side. Keep the toast and the red knob.
- p03: Cookie Jar: Replace the marble with a smooth painted wooden counter in a warm mid tan, slightly darker than the jar. Give the jar a soft glaze with one small highlight on the upper left and a slightly deeper warm shadow on its right side, with a short contact shadow under it. Keep the cow face, cookies and red lid.
- p04: Kettle: Replace the purple gradient with a violet radial sunburst of light and mid violet rays. Replace the sharp white glints with one broad soft sheen on the upper left, and make the right side of the body a slightly deeper wine red. Make the handle dark grey instead of black.
- p05: Faucet: Make the wall tiles a smooth light teal with soft, faint grout lines, and remove the yellow sunbeam streaks. Make the chrome a soft satin with one bright highlight down the left of the neck and a slightly darker right side. Keep the drops, the blue knob and the sink.
- p06: Whisk: Replace the blurry green field and light shafts with an even green radial sunburst of light and mid green rays. Give the wires a soft silver sheen, brighter on the left wires and slightly darker on the right. Keep the orange handle.
- p08: keep
- p09: Rolling Pin: Replace the white marble counter with a smooth painted wooden counter in a mid tone, slightly darker than the dough. Make the wood grain on the pin fine and faint, and keep only a light dusting of flour. Keep the red handles and the purple glow.
- p10: keep
- p11: Oven Mitts: Replace the dashed stitching around the house patch with a soft solid seam. Make the orange fabric smooth with no fuzz, keeping the quilted diamonds as soft shallow puffs. Keep the mitts, colors and backdrop otherwise.
