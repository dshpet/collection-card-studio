## Art director

Yes, this is close. The palette, hero rendering and backdrop patterns already sit near the real cards, and two cards (jack-o'-lantern, candelabra) can stay. The tombstone needs only a light cleanup. The tells that remain are mostly staging and surface: heavy textured ground, the bat's cute face and vinyl finish, evenly spread fine marks, and a few story extras. The notes stay small so the look does not swing away from this.

Overruled:
- All shape_proportions fixes, and the viewpoint and composition fixes (turn, enlarge, crop), because form cannot be judged across different objects and those changes are outside the allowed notes.
- The specialists who called the column (p04) or the ship (p12) generated. Both are real. So a crack, a plaque, a plum wall, a tiled floor and busy wave crests are all within the real look, and a dark background is not a fault on its own.
- The picks of the real topiary (edges), the real lamp (lighting, colour, edges) and the real door's measured density.
- Darkening or muting the backgrounds in general. The real cards use loud pure fields too, so that would be over-correction.

1. **The ground under the hero is a heavy textured material where real cards use a plain surface. This shows on the cracked rock slab (cauldron), the dark grained shelf with a black band (crystal ball) and the mottled flagstones (coffin). It is the strongest tell because it pulls the eye from the hero and adds a dark mass real cards lack.** Stand the hero on a smooth plain ledge or simple floorboards in one soft colour, with one clean front edge.
2. **Invented character and one coating on the hero. The bat has a cute open-mouthed face, and its body, wings and bones share one glossy vinyl finish with a shiny rim traced along the wing edges.** Keep the bat a plain animal: closed mouth, small dark eyes, short matte fur on the body and soft matte wing skin with no shiny edge line.
3. **Fine marks are spread evenly over surfaces: pits and stipple on the tombstone, dark contour and scratch hatching on the house, flame-figured varnished grain on the coffin, feathery streaks in the crystal ball. Real cards keep large smooth planes with a few marks where wear belongs.** Use smooth surfaces with soft shading, thin seams and only a few marks at worn edges. Separate parts by light and shadow rather than dark drawn lines.
4. **Story scenery and a hero-copying backdrop. Moon and twisted trees around the house, sunset clouds and distant tree clumps behind the pumpkins, and broom icons as wallpaper behind the broom. Real props are allowed, as the real column's plaque and the real mower's daisies show, so remove only what competes with the hero or copies it.** Use a clear sky or an abstract tone-on-tone pattern behind the hero, with a few large quiet shapes.
5. **Light does not behave on some heroes. Glowing windows light nothing around them, the crystal ball has no glass shading, and shadows stay warm or thin where real cards fall into a soft cool shadow.** Use one soft light direction, a soft cool shadow side, and let glowing parts spill warm light onto nearby surfaces.

- p01: Bat: close the mouth and show only two tiny fangs, with small plain dark eyes. Give the body short matte fur and the wings soft matte skin. Remove the thin shiny line along the wing edges.
- p02: Cauldron: replace the cracked rock slab with a smooth plain ledge in soft plum-grey, with one clean front edge. Add a slight deep blue-green sheen to the iron belly. Keep the green brew and the wall.
- p03: Pumpkins: change the sunset to a clear soft blue sky with no clouds and no distant tree clumps. Add a soft blue-tinted shadow on the grass to one side of the group, and make the far sides of the pumpkins slightly cooler and darker.
- p05: Tombstone: make the stone face smooth with very soft grain, removing the pits and speckle. Remove the shiny spot on the upper-left shoulder. Keep everything else.
- p06: Broom: replace the broom-icon wallpaper with soft diagonal stripes in two close teals. Make the straw dry and matte with a soft darker shadow side. Make the floor shadow slightly shorter and softer.
- p07: Haunted house: remove the moon and the two bare trees, leaving plain blue sky with a few soft clouds. Replace the dark drawn lines on walls and roof with soft shading and thin seams. Let the window glow spill slightly onto the frames and nearby stone.
- p08: keep
- p09: Crystal ball: replace the dark wooden shelf with a smooth plain ledge in a lighter orange tone, with no grain and no dark band. Make the mist inside one smooth soft swirl. Add a slightly darker glass rim and a warm glow inside the lower edge.
- p10: Coffin: replace the flagstones with plain smooth floorboards in one warm tone. Soften the wall stripes to two close greens. Make the wood grain fine, straight and quiet under a satin finish.
- p11: keep
