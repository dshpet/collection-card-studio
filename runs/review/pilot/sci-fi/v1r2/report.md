## Art director

Yes, this is close. The floating single-object cards on bold patterned backdrops (ray gun, telescope, UFO, rocket) could sit beside the real cards, and I kept all four. The hero objects are mostly convincing; the remaining tells are in staging. In order, those are the grey floors on p03 and p11, the invented atmosphere on p04 and p05 (with smaller versions on p01 and p08), and the soft shadowless light on p01, p03, p04 and p11. Six cards get small edits.

The style text is unchanged, so these go in as notes only.

I overruled several specialists:
- Shape and viewpoint fixes are dropped, because form can't be judged across different objects. So are the composition fixes that re-pose or enlarge a hero.
- The pattern of 'dull the backgrounds' is overruled. The real spyglass and audio guide cards are just as loud, so muting them would over-correct.
- Lighting, value, edges and staging all blamed the helm on p07, and it is real. Its pale rays and cracked deck are not tells, and the telescope needs no change.
- Value and surface blamed the real submarine on p12, and value and edges flagged the real jacket on p06. Those darker corners are on-model, so nobody should ask for flat fields.
- Shape, value and color flags on the control pairs p06 and p09 are noise, and surface and edges calls are mostly weak.

1. **Dead neutral grey floor under the hero (p03, p11). It ignores the backdrop color, meets the wall at a hard straight line, and is the strongest tell on the sheet. On p03 the grey console merges into it. On p11 the white helmet sits in a dull band. Real ledges carry the backdrop's color or are a real material, like the paving on p11 A and the wood deck on p07.** Draw the floor as a soft ledge in a lighter tint of the backdrop color, with the backdrop's color falling onto it and a soft transition where floor meets wall.
2. **Invented atmosphere the object does not need (p04, p05, p08, and the sky on p01): neon strips and floor rings, sparkles, nebula clouds, extra moons, tiny themed icons, a magenta-to-cyan sky. These add small bright marks and compete with the hero. Real cards use a few large quiet shapes behind it.** Put the hero on a simple backdrop of large quiet shapes: one calm color, a plain pattern or a plain wall, with the hero as the only busy thing on the card.
3. **Soft, even, near-frontal light with no committed dark side (p01, p03, p04, p11). The form rounds off like clay and the shadow stays faint. The real cards in these pairs show a light side, a darker side and a firm contact or cast shadow, as on the p04 boat and the p01 gate piers.** Light from the upper left. Let the lower right of the object fall into a cool, deeper shadow, with a dark contact shadow at the base and a soft cast shadow to one side.
4. **Slightly over-defined small parts: bright rims and tick marks on p03, screws on p08. This is a minor tell. Only a few cards show it, and many of the calls in these two reports are weak.** Where a note touches a card, keep fine marks quiet and let them fade. No global change.

- p01: Change the magenta and purple sky to one deep blue sky. Calm the planet: fewer white clouds, slightly darker and quieter. Shade the lower right of the hull slightly darker and cooler.
- p02: keep
- p03: Change the grey floor to a soft ledge in a lighter orange tint of the wall, with a soft line where floor meets wall. Darken the right side of the console slightly and add a dark contact shadow under the base. Soften the bright rim along the top edge.
- p04: Replace the glowing strips, lit pillars and neon floor rings with one plain curved wall in a calm mid teal and a plain softly shaded floor. Light the pod from the upper left, with the right third falling into cool shadow. Add a soft shadow under the feet.
- p05: Remove the two small moons, the sparkle stars and the pink clouds. Put the planet on a plain deep blue field with a soft tone-on-tone pattern. Deepen the lower right of the planet into a cool shadow and let the ring cast a soft dark band across it.
- p07: keep
- p08: Replace the small gear and star icons with a smooth orange field, slightly brighter behind the robot. Leave the robot as it is.
- p10: keep
- p11: Change the grey floor to a soft ledge in a lighter green tint of the backdrop, with a soft line where floor meets wall. Slightly deepen the green behind the helmet so the white shell stands out. Light from the upper left, with the right side of the shell in cool shadow and the floor shadow falling to one side.
- p12: keep
