## Art director

Close in subject, palette, framing and backgrounds, and it often fools the specialists: 8 of 9 picked the real whistle as generated. It would not sit beside the real cards unnoticed, though. The generator has overshot into crisp, near-photographic detail with hard corners, rivets, stitching and mirror metal, while the real cards are softer, simpler and rounder. The current style text drives much of this, so the replacement reverses it while keeping thin parts slim and materials readable. Taxi, cook and tent are furthest off; whistle and teacup are closest. No card is kept. Surface finish was right on 8 of 8 pairs and lighting on 1 of 8, so lighting's crisp-plane and no-glow fixes are overruled.

1. **Everything is rendered too crisp and too real. Fine detail is spread evenly over every surface: speckle, pitting, scratches, fibre strands, dashed stitching, gravel and tooled leather. This is the biggest tell, and the current style text causes it by asking for 'fine real detail: stitching, grain, seams, rivets'. Real cards are softer and quieter. Surface finish called all 8 pairs correctly.** Paint smooth, soft surfaces. Give each material a few quiet marks that show what it is, such as a few faint grain lines or simple rope twists. Keep the sharpest detail on the main shape and let everything else stay calm.
2. **Rigid objects look engineered, with hard corners, tight bevels and flat machined faces. The real whistle, taxi, tent and pot are soft and gently rounded, and the real basket has big apples and a fat rim. Overruled: shape_proportions on p01 and p04, lighting_shading's 'crisp planes' fixes on most pairs, and viewpoint_depth's flat-face fixes. Those reports describe the generator's habit as if it were the target.** Draw soft, smooth 3D forms with gently rounded edges and simple broad shapes. Plastic, paint and cloth turn slowly from light to shade.
3. **Invented hardware and props: rows of rivets, deck nails, rope lashings, stone rings, pebbles, corner flowers, ferns, a boulder, sunbeams, dappled leaf shadow, grommets, strap toggles and bumper overriders. The real cards use far fewer parts.** Build the subject from its few essential parts. Keep the setting open: a few large soft shapes around the subject, with clear space beside it.
4. **Secondary parts come out enlarged and the hero is pushed toward the frame on p02, p07 and p08: a huge bell, heavy tassels, thick stakes, fat logs and an oversized pot. This is not universal, because the generated basket on p04 is smaller than the real one. It is a thin-part rule, not an order to shrink every hero.** Keep thin parts slim: poles, pegs, cords, handles and mouthpieces. Main bodies stay full and rounded.
5. **Gloss is too hard. Metal gets mirror chrome, near-black streaks and white hot spots, and glaze gets sharp reflection arcs. Real metal and glaze show one calm, continuous gradient with one soft highlight.** Give each object one smooth, soft highlight. Paint metal as a calm, soft gradient.
6. **Backgrounds are harder than the real ones: flat saturated fields and a sharp two-colour horizon on p03. Overruled: nearly every specialist treated a glow behind the hero as a generated tell, but the real whistle has that glow, the real basket has a pale washed wall and a table reflection, and the real teacup sits in soft shadow with no horizon. Do not ban glow. It stays a card note because it does not hold on every pair, since generated p06 also has a bloom.** Handle this per card: soft backgrounds with gentle light behind the object, and walls that melt into the surface.
7. **Local colour drifts on some cards: orange wood on p05, an extra orange tent trim on p07, loud blue on p03 and neon cyan on p02. Overruled as a global rule: color_palette's call to mute everything would be over-correction, because the real whistle, apples, basket and taxi are as saturated as the generated ones or more so.** Handle this per card only. Add no general instruction to mute colour.

Style text:

The attached images are real cards from the game. Draw one new card that could sit beside them unnoticed: the same rendering, lighting, finish, color and level of detail.
Draw soft, smooth 3D forms with gently rounded edges and simple broad shapes. Each material gets a few quiet marks and one smooth, soft highlight. Metal is a calm, soft gradient. Thin parts stay slim: poles, pegs, cords, handles.
Keep the setting open and calm: a few large soft shapes around the subject, with clear space beside it.
Draw only what the subject names. No text, letters, numbers, frame, border or watermark.

- p01: Soften the whole whistle into smooth, satin moulded plastic with rounded edges around the mouth, window and side disc. Clean, grain-free surface. Lighter yellow background with a gentle pale glow around the whistle, and softer campfire icons on an even grid.
- p02: Make the bell smaller and turn the bugle more side-on, with a long tube narrowing to a small mouthpiece. Paint the brass as one smooth gradient with one soft highlight. Small tassels on thin cords, drawn as soft clumps, with simple rope twists. Softer, paler turquoise rays, and clear space on every side.
- p03: Lower the camera so the tea is a thin ellipse and the cup sits low in the saucer. Soft creamy porcelain with a muted powder-blue rim and a soft, simple flower with olive leaves. Plain calm tea. One muted red background that melts softly into the surface, with a long quiet shadow.
- p04: Use a few big, smooth apples filling the top of the basket, with a soft blended blush and one broad sheen on each. Fat soft rim, wide simple weave and a chunky soft handle. Plain smooth cloth. Pale washed lilac wall with light behind the basket, and a warm soft reflection on the table.
- p05: Straighter barrel with a gentle belly, seen from a lower camera so only a thin sliver of the top shows. Matte brown wood varied from tan to red-brown with a few faint grain lines. Plain wide metal hoops with a soft highlight. Smooth deck with no nail heads.
- p06: Soft rounded toy-like car with flowing body panels and a broad gentle sheen on the paint. Satin metal bumper and a simple grille, small rounded mirrors, simple seats and tyres. Smooth matte road with large clean lane dashes, and even blue rays.
- p07: One ochre-yellow canvas tent with large soft folds, a plain hem and soft rolled door flaps. Thin lines running to tiny pegs. Open, quiet lawn with a broad plain foreground, tall trees further back and tiny specks of flowers at most. Clear space on both sides of the tent.
- p08: Smaller plain grey pot with a thin rim and a thin wire handle, soft shading, and a gentle warm glow low on its base. Slim upright poles with forked tops and a slim crossbar, no rope. Small simple fire with a few rounded logs, on a flat dirt patch with a few small flat stones. Soup with a few large simple ingredients.
