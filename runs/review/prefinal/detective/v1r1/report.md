## Art director

The set is fairly close. The magnifier and the safe could pass as real, and the fedora and shield are near misses. Most remaining failures come from two habits. First, a tabletop scene on dark polished wood with fine, photo-like grain (typewriter, phone, folder). Second, a product-photo finish on dark or metal objects: glossy black, chrome hits and texture grain (camera, typewriter, phone, shield). Smaller tells are a glow behind the hero on graphic backdrops (dog, camera), near-black heroes against pale walls (door), and blade-level grass. Every shape_proportions call is overruled, and so are the many specialist picks against the real pump, ticket, sneakers and oil lamp. Seven of the ten generated cards get edit notes; the magnifier and safe are kept as they are.

1. **Dark polished wood desk filling the lower half of the card. Typewriter, phone and folder all sit on near-black mahogany or walnut with fine, photo-like grain. This pulls the whole card warm and dark and is the strongest tell in this set. The real book and gloves sit on light honey or orange wood painted as a few broad stripes.** For tabletop cards, use a light warm honey-orange wooden surface painted as a few wide, soft grain bands, in the light-to-middle values, with a soft, light cast shadow.
2. **Product-photo rendering of dark or metal heroes: glossy black bodies, mirror chrome, sharp white specular hits, near-black shadow sides, and photographic material grain (leatherette, brushed-metal streaks, felt fuzz). Seen on the typewriter, phone, box camera, shield and fedora. Real cards show smooth satin surfaces with broad soft highlights and a shadow side that stays mid-toned.** Paint surfaces as smooth satin or matte with one or two broad soft highlights from the upper left. Keep shadow sides a medium, cool tone of the local colour, and keep metal a soft satin sheen.
3. **Bright glow or halo hugging the hero on graphic or striped backdrops (dog, box camera). The real sunbursts and stripes keep an even tone right up to the hero.** Keep sunburst, ring and stripe backdrops evenly lit all the way to the centre, with only a gentle lighter centre and no bright patch behind the object.
4. **Hero as a near-black mass against a pale backdrop (office door, box camera). On real cards the hero and the backdrop sit within moderate value steps of each other.** Give dark objects a clear mid-value local colour, such as warm brown or deep red, with lighter lit faces.
5. **Blade-level or grain-level micro detail in the floor or backdrop (single grass blades, fine wood grain). Real cards paint materials as a few broad, quiet shapes.** Paint grass, wood and other ground materials as a few broad, soft shapes with gentle shading.
6. **Overruled: every shape_proportions call. Form cannot be compared across different objects, and the sneakers, ticket and pump it flagged are all real. Also overruled: the many specialists who picked the real fuel pump (p03A), ticket (p11B), sneakers (p08A) and oil lamp (p07A). The viewpoint call against the safe's projection and the edge and outline calls on the magnifier also have no visible basis.** No change.
