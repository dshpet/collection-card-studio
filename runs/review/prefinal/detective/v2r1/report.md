## Art director

Close. The objects, rendering, scene staging and backdrops already match the real cards, and the specialists' over-staging and halo calls are overruled by the controls (the ship and the planter). What still gives cards away is value and finish: dark wood tables (wallet, bag, map), fine surface noise (stitching, brushed metal, inked map lines, a grainy fingerprint), and lighting that is too hard on the car and the camera. Keep 3 (flashlight, bloodhound, fedora); edit 7 with small value, light and finish changes.

1. **Dark red-brown or near-black wood tabletops push the whole card into a low key. The real cards sit in a bright mid-light key, and their wood is warm honey or orange (p06 B binoculars, the travel book). This is the strongest giveaway on p04, p12, and the corners of p06.** Put tabletop objects on light, warm honey-orange wooden planks in bright daylight, so the table is lighter than the object on it.
2. **Fine surface noise that real cards don't have: dashed stitching and pebbled leather (p04), brushed-metal streaks (p08), inked dark street lines on the map (p06), a grainy fingerprint swirl (p10), speckled asphalt (p02), and many small wavy plastic highlights (p12). Real cards use broad, smooth sheens and quiet grain.** Draw surfaces as smooth materials with one or two broad soft highlights and only faint grain or seams.
3. **Lighting misses the real cards' soft upper-left key in two ways. Some cards are hard and theatrical, with long, dark, sharp cast shadows and near-black shadow sides (p02 car, p08 camera). Others are flat with no light side, like the p12 bag. Real cards have a soft key, a gently darker right side and a short, soft contact shadow.** Light the object from the upper left with soft light. Make the right side gently darker and give it a short, soft, pale contact shadow.
4. **A photographic studio-product look: a spotlight vignette, a long streaked cast shadow and a glossy plastic sheen (p03 tape). The real cards' plain fields stay evenly lit and illustrated.** Keep plain backdrops evenly toned to the corners, with a soft, short shadow under the object.
5. **OVERRULED: 'over-staging' and 'diorama' calls (street, garden, ivy, waves, props) and 'glow halo behind the hero' calls. The control cards contradict them. The real ship has foam strokes and a pink glow, the real planter has a fence, bushes, a watering can and a trowel, the real lamp has a yellow glow behind it, and the real door has dense ivy. Proportion and roundness fixes are also out of scope because the objects differ, and the p07 and p11 picks were wrong.** No change: full scenes, partner props and a soft glow behind the hero are allowed.

- p01: keep
- p02: Police Car: Replace the long dark cast shadow with a soft, short, pale contact shadow under the wheels, and make the car's right side only slightly darker than the front. Make the road a smooth, slightly lighter grey-violet asphalt with no speckle. Keep the black-and-white car, the red light, the houses and the sky.
- p03: Tape Roll: Make the green backdrop one even mid green from centre to corners, with no dark vignette. Replace the long cast shadow with a short, soft contact shadow under the roll and the strip. Make the yellow tape slightly satin with one broad soft highlight; keep the cardboard core and the loose strip.
- p04: Badge Wallet: Lay the wallet on light, warm honey-orange wooden planks in bright daylight, in place of the dark brown wood. Make the leather smooth and softly shaded, with no stitch dashes and no pebbled grain. Keep the gold badge, the card and the pocket.
- p05: keep
- p06: Map Magnifier: Make the table under the map a light, warm honey wood in place of the dark red-brown. Draw the map streets and blocks as pale flat colour shapes with no dark lines, and make the paper slightly smoother. Keep the gold magnifier, the green park and the clear lens.
- p08: Flash Camera: Make the flash dish and top plate smooth polished silver with broad soft highlights and no brushed streaks. Light the camera softly from the upper left, so the black body's right side is dark grey with a soft cool edge sheen, not near-black. Keep the red button, the lens and the blue rings.
- p09: keep
- p10: Door Knob: Make the green door a slightly lighter, brighter mid green. Draw the fingerprint as a smooth, soft pale swirl with no grain. Light the knob from the upper left with one broad soft sheen and a gently darker lower right; keep the brass plate, the screws and the room glimpse.
- p12: Evidence Bag: Set the bag on light, warm honey-orange wooden planks in bright daylight, in place of the dark wood. Light it softly from the upper left, with two or three broad clean highlight bands on the plastic and a soft contact shadow under its edges. Keep the gold ring and the blue zip strip.
