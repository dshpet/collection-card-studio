## Art director

Close. Rocket, Space Gloves and Helmet could pass as real cards. Jetpack is near: it needs only a light value and finish touch. The clearest tells are the photographic joystick counter and foil pouch, then invented hardware and glow extras (porthole, UFO, capsule) and colourless white or grey heroes (observatory, capsule). Framing is too pulled back on some cards, but past framing notes lost with the judge, so the notes stick to colour, finish and extras.

1. **Photographic product-shot materials. The joystick stands on a brushed-steel counter in a box-room corner, and the pouch is crinkled real foil on a white studio floor with a long cast shadow. Nothing in the real set looks like a photo, so these two give themselves away fastest.** Stand objects on a plain floor tinted the same colour as the wall, or on smooth cartoon wood. Paint metal and foil as smooth satin with a few broad soft folds or one broad highlight.
2. **Invented small hardware and glow extras: a hex-bolt ring, riveted wall plates and glowing light strips around the porthole, rivet rows and a screwed hatch panel on the capsule, rim-light halos and a light beam under the UFO, and flower specks and grass tufts on the observatory lawn. On the real cards, hardware is a few broad smooth bands and backdrops stay quiet.** Draw rims and seams as one or two wide smooth bands. Keep the walls, lawns and sky plain and soft, and draw lights as small solid bulbs.
3. **Heroes with almost no colour of their own: grey tanks, a silver pouch, a white observatory and a white capsule. Their shadows pick up the backdrop's hue (cyan, violet, green) instead of a darker shade of their own colour. Real heroes carry their own saturated colour and contrast warm against cool with the backdrop.** Give white or metal props a warm local colour on their main areas, such as sandstone, copper, or an orange label, and keep the white for trim. Shade the white with soft neutral cool grey.
4. **Values pushed to the extremes inside the hero: the near-black joystick base and the near-black capsule heat shield sit against near-white bodies, and the capsule is lit very one-sidedly. Real cards keep their darks small and in the middle range.** Bring the darkest hero parts up to a medium tone with a lit top edge, and keep shadow sides a gentle mid-tone.
5. **Pulled-back framing, with the whole object small and centred (observatory, capsule, rocket). This is real, but past framing edges were rejected by the judge, so I am not putting crops in the notes this round. I overruled all shape, proportion and roundness findings, since form cannot be compared across different objects. I also overruled findings against known real cards: surface_finish on the locker, boxing gloves, phone booth and sneakers, lighting on the locker, value on the bike and submarine, edges on the airplane, and viewpoint on the seahorse and hoop.** No change in this round.

- p01: Joystick: Replace the brushed-steel counter with a plain floor in the same mid green as the walls, with no metal streaks and no hard front edge. Make the base a slightly lighter charcoal-grey plastic with a soft highlight along its top rim. Keep the red ball, the yellow button and the green room.
- p02: Jetpack: Make the tanks a slightly brighter satin silver, with one broad highlight on the left of each and a slightly darker right side. Lighten the dark green corners of the burst slightly so they are only gently darker than the centre. Keep the orange nozzles, the straps and the green burst.
- p03: keep
- p04: Boxing Gloves (pouch card): Make the foil a smooth satin silver with two or three broad soft folds and one broad highlight, in place of the fine crinkles. Add a bright orange printed label with a simple fruit on the front. Tint the floor a slightly deeper cyan than the stripes, with no white and a short soft shadow. Keep the red cap and the striped wall.
- p07: keep
- p08: Lighthouse (porthole card): Remove the glowing light strips and the riveted wall plates, leaving a smooth pink wall with one or two faint seams. Make the steel rim a soft satin, with one broad highlight on the upper left and a slightly darker lower right, and draw the bolts as small, soft bumps. Keep the navy window with its stars.
- p09: keep
- p10: Park Gate (observatory card): Remove the white flower specks and grass tufts so the lawn is smooth. Paint the stone wall a warm pale sandstone and the dome a soft teal-grey, keeping white only on the ledge trim. Keep the red door, the slit, the trees and the sky.
- p11: Submarine (UFO card): Remove the light beam and the glow under the saucer, so the yellow burst is even. Draw the rim lights as small solid warm bulbs with no halos. Keep the purple saucer, the green dome and the yellow burst.
- p12: Space Capsule: Remove the rivets and the screwed-on hatch panel, leaving one or two smooth seams. Make the heat shield a medium copper-brown with a lit top edge, not near-black. Lift the right side of the cone slightly to a soft mid grey-blue. Keep the yellow window, the sky and the clouds.
