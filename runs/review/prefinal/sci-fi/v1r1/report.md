## Art director

Mid-way there. The rendering, shapes and charm match the real cards, and the helmet, rocket and UFO pass as they are. Most of the others give themselves away through colour and material. They sit on grey or white settings with grey objects (chip, vault, console, dish), use photoreal brushed steel, concrete and foil, or put a glow halo behind the object (flask, satellite). Fixing the backdrops and making surfaces smoother and more matte should bring most cards close, without changing form or framing.

1. **Grey and white settings with colourless heroes. The chip sits on an off-white sheet, the vault in a white wall, the console is grey on a grey counter, and the dish is white on grey concrete. Every real card puts a hero with its own colour against a saturated, opposite-temperature backdrop. At thumbnail size this is the biggest giveaway.** Put every object on a saturated coloured backdrop or surface. Give the object at least one clear local colour that differs from the backdrop's hue: a warm object on a cool ground, or the reverse.
2. **Photoreal surface materials real cards don't use: brushed-steel counters with mirror reflections, speckled concrete, crinkled foil, rows of small rivets and screws. The brushed-steel counter on a flat wall is also a recurring stock set (flask, console).** Paint surfaces as smooth, soft, mostly matte colour. Show hardware as broad smooth bands, and give each object only one or two broad highlights. Ground and tabletops are plain smooth colour with at most faint wide grain.
3. **A bright glow or spotlight halo behind the object (flask wall, satellite burst). Real ray and wall backdrops stay even, with at most a soft brighter area low in the centre.** Keep backdrops evenly lit, with no bright spot directly behind the object.
4. **Neon pure-hue local colour (lime liquid) and shadows tinted with the backdrop's hue (violet rover). Real cards shade each colour with a deeper version of itself.** Use rich but natural colours, and shade each colour with a darker version of itself.
5. **Overruled: the shape/proportion fixes (form can't be compared across different objects). Also overruled: the reframing pushes on cards that are fine, such as the helmet, rocket and UFO, because real cards like the claw machine and ring box also show whole, centred objects. Lighting and edges mostly flagged real cards (sneakers, balloons, locker, gate, bike), so those findings don't point at a tell either.** No change to style for these.

- p05: Vehicle: Remove the scattered rocks and pebbles so the ground is smooth with two or three large soft craters. - p09: Control Panel: Make the counter a plain matte mid-grey with no brushed streaks or reflections. Paint the console body a soft warm cream-yellow with a slightly darker right side. Keep the radar screen, gauges, levers, red button and blue wall.
- p11: Fan: Radar Dish: Make the rooftop a smooth warm sandstone colour with no speckle and a soft-edged shadow. Make the sky a slightly deeper blue. Paint the mount column a soft orange-red with the dish staying white; keep the red tip.
- p12: Honor Medal: Satellite: Make the gold body a smooth gold box with two or three broad soft folds instead of crinkled foil. Change the green burst to even deep blue rays with no bright glow behind the satellite. Keep the blue solar panels and the dish.
