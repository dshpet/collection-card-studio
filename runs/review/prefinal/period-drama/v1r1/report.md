## Art director

Close. Each generated card sits in the right genre, palette family and backdrop vocabulary. The specialists split often and flagged the real cards on p01, p09 and several B sides, so most generated cards could pass at a glance. The tells I can see are small, crisp surface detail (lace, eyelets, velvet speckle, brushed brass, mirror gold, brick joints, tea foam), whites that drift cream or lavender, and on the Front Door a pulled-back scene with invented corner flowers and petals. The Front Door, Lace Gloves and pocket watch give themselves away the most. The envelope, top hat and teacup are near passes and need only light notes.

1. **Fine, crisp surface detail and all-over gloss. Generated cards draw small sharp detail that real cards smooth away: eyelet holes on the parasol frill, all-over lace mesh on the gloves, speckle on the velvet, brushed streaks on the gramophone horn, mirror-gold watch with knurling and heavy numerals, foam bubbles on the tea, sharp brick joints. They also add mirror floors. This is the clearest giveaway on the sheet. The real cards (cooler, book, umbrella, fan, rocking chair, vending machine) use broad matte areas with one or two soft highlights.** Per card: replace fine pattern with smooth matte or satin surfaces and one broad soft highlight. Keep large motifs and leave out small repeated ones.
2. **Whites drift to cream or pick up the background's tint. Examples: cream envelope, cream feather, cream bricks, a lavender teacup, pink-tinted glove shadows. The whole card then sits in one warm family, or the hero shares the backdrop's hue. Real whites (cooler lid, book page edge) stay clean and cool-neutral.** Per card: make whites clean cool white with pale grey or grey-blue shading. Keep the existing backdrop colour unless it repeats the hero's hue.
3. **Pulled-back scene with invented extras. The front door is a small slab in a wide brick wall, with bougainvillea in the corners, bushes, paving and fallen petals. Real cards keep the surroundings to a few large, quiet shapes.** Front Door only: remove the corner flowers and petals and quiet the wall. Do not attempt a recrop through an edit.
4. **Light that misses the steady top-left key. Some cards are lit flat from the front (envelope, armchair, watch). Others are pushed too hard: the quill's long near-black cast shadow, the near-black top hat, the gramophone's dark corners. Real cards turn into a medium shadow on the right and use soft contact shadows.** Per card: say 'slightly darker right side' or 'softer, shorter shadow'. Do not ask for dramatic relighting.
5. **Overruled findings. The shape specialist's swelling claims cannot be judged across different objects, and form changes are banned. Several specialists flagged the real cards: the p01 pool, p09 spotlight and coral, p02 book stickers, p04 fan flatness and p12 vending machine. The viewpoint objections to the fan, watch and hat are weak, because those cards read fine in the game's flat-icon style.** Ignore these findings. No notes on proportion, thickness or camera.

- p02: Sealed Letter: Make the envelope paper a slightly cooler off-white with soft pale grey shading along the bottom edge and the right side, in place of the cream. Give it a soft cast shadow on the wood to the lower right. Keep the red wax seal and the wooden planks.
- p03: Parasol: Draw the pink frill as a plain soft ruffle with smooth fabric and no punched lace holes. Shade the underside of the canopy and the frill folds in a slightly darker, cooler tone. Keep the yellow dome, the wooden crook handle and the blue rings.
- p04: Lace Gloves: Make the gloves smooth white satin with only a few large, soft lace flowers near the cuffs and no mesh weave. Shade them with soft cool grey on the undersides and the right edges. Make the pink background slightly calmer and less hot; keep the blue bows and the wave pattern.
- p05: Velvet Chair: Make the upholstery a smooth matte red fabric with broad soft shading and no fine speckle. Let the cushion tops and arm rolls catch a slightly brighter red from the upper left, with a slightly darker right side. Make the floor a satin wood with faint grain and soft, blurred reflections; keep the gold buttons, the legs and the striped wall.
- p06: Top Hat: Make the hat a deep charcoal plum-black with a broad soft highlight down the lit left side of the crown and along the top of the brim, keeping the darkest black only under the brim. Keep the red band, the shape and the blue burst.
- p07: Quill Pen: Make the feather a clean cool white with pale grey-blue shading in place of the cream. Make the inkwell's cast shadow slightly shorter, softer and lighter. Keep the green inkwell, the striped yellow wall and the table.
- p08: Gramophone: Make the horn smooth brass with one or two broad soft highlights and no brushed streaks. Make the table a satin wood with faint grain and soft, blurred reflections. Lighten the purple corners slightly to a medium violet; keep the record, the box and the crank.
- p10: Honor Medal: Make the gold case a soft satin gold with one broad highlight on the upper left and a slightly darker right side, in place of the mirror shine. Print the numerals slightly smaller in soft dark brown and make the crown a smooth knob. Make the green diamond background slightly deeper and calmer; keep the chain and the dial.
- p11: Front Door: Remove the pink bougainvillea in the top corners and the fallen petals, leaving plain wall. Paint the brick wall a soft cool grey-beige with faint, low-contrast joints and soften the leaf shadows. Keep the green door, the fanlight, the steps and the two flower bushes.
- p12: Vending: Shade the cup and saucer in clean neutral white with soft cool grey shadows and a slightly darker right side, in place of the lavender tint. Draw the tea as a smooth amber surface without the ring of foam bubbles. Keep the blue rims, the steam and the purple background.
