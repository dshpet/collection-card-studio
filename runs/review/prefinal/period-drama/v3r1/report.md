## Art director

Close. Half the generated cards (gift, parasol, teacup, fan, chest) would pass as real: the specialists mostly flagged the real card as odd in those pairs, and in the controls they flagged a real card too. The cards that give themselves away share one root cause: photographic materials (linen weave, velvet, mahogany figure, violin grain, mirror gloss), often with a neutral black or white hero pushed to value extremes. The pearls on dark velvet are the clearest miss, then the cake stand, piano, violin and top hat. The edit notes target only finish, colour and value. Framing and form stay as drawn.

1. **Photographic material rendering: real-world texture (linen weave, velvet nap, swirling mahogany figure, violin flame grain) and mirror-like gloss, where real cards paint smooth, simplified surfaces with one broad soft sheen. This gives away the pearls, cake stand, piano and violin more than anything else.** Paint every material as smooth stylised paint: wood as satin with at most a few faint long grain lines, cloth as smooth fabric with a few large soft folds, porcelain and lacquer as satin with one broad soft highlight. Show floor reflections as soft and blurred.
2. **Neutral heroes pushed to black and white extremes: white pearls on near-black navy velvet, a solid black glossy top hat, a white stand on a white cloth under a dark wall, black violin fittings. Real cards keep the hero a saturated local colour, with coloured mid-dark shadows and deep darks only in small accents.** Give black or white parts a colour: blacks become deep plum, navy or warm brown with soft mid-tone planes on the lit side, and whites become cream or pastel with soft coloured shadows. Keep the backdrop at a mid value so the hero separates from it.
3. **Overruled: most specialists picked the real card as the odd one in several pairs (sneakers, soccer ball, suitcase, ring box, gate, guitar, purple fan, scarf, bugle), so their fixes for those cards, and their 'generated pattern' claims built on them, do not apply. Real cards also use centred whole objects, stage and garden scenes, and busy floors. Composition, viewpoint and shape notes are dropped because form and framing are out of scope here and the real cards show no consistent 3/4-crop rule.** No change. The current framing, setting density and object construction match the real cards closely enough.
