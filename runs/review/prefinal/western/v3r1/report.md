## Art director

Close. The generated cards have the game's palette, glossy toy forms and backdrop types (rings, rays, diamonds, stripes), and the cowboy hat and cactus could pass as they are. One main thing gives the rest away: fine surface detail. Leather has stitching, wood has grain, metal has bolts and chrome, and the tumbleweed has bark texture, where the real cards stay broad and smooth. Two cards also add staged extras: the campfire's cabin, kettle and night scene, and the windmill's plinth. The lantern and the belt buckle use hard black or chrome metal. All three fixes are finish and staging changes, so small edits should be enough.

1. **Surface micro-detail on materials: dashed stitch lines and pebbled grain on leather (belt, boots, saddle), streaky wood grain and rings of bolt heads on wood and iron (wagon wheel, windmill), bark streaks and split ends on the tumbleweed. The real cards (grad cap, boxing gloves, viewer, submarine) are broad, smooth, softly shaded shapes. This is the strongest tell and affects 6 of 10 generated cards.** Draw leather as smooth, soft leather with a gentle satin sheen and simple raised edges. Draw wood as smooth, softly shaded colour with at most a few faint wide grain lines. Draw iron and steel as plain smooth bands with only a few rivets.
2. **Staged extras and bases around the hero: the campfire comes with a log cabin, a kettle on a stone, scattered pebbles and a black night, and the windmill stands on a stone plinth on a dirt patch with pebbles. Real cards keep a single hero against a quiet backdrop. I overrule the calls on the topiary and the cactus: the real topiary card sits on a planter with a path and hedge, so a modest setting is in style.** Show only the hero object with its ground continuing naturally under it, and a soft, simple backdrop behind it.
3. **Hard metal finish: mirror-chrome rims with white hot spots (belt buckle) and near-black iron with crisp white edge highlights and heavy dark contours (lantern). Real metal (viewer, submarine trim) is satin with broad, soft sheens and coloured shadows.** Paint metal as soft satin with one broad highlight and a slightly darker, coloured shadow side.
4. **Overruled and not acted on. Shape_proportions: form cannot be compared across objects, and the inflated toy forms match the real gloves, mower and submarine. The value specialist's glow-halo pattern: the real oil lamp, mower and ice-cream cards also have a glow behind the hero. Lighting's frontal-gloss and color's warm-cast patterns: most of their calls landed on real cards (beach ball, sneakers, gloves, mower, submarine), so the patterns are not supported.** No change.

- p01: Tumbleweed: Make the twigs smooth warm brown with soft matte shading, with no bark streaks, grain lines or split ends. Keep the purple backdrop, the sand and the shadow.
- p02: Belt: Make the leather smooth soft brown with a gentle satin sheen and no stitch lines along the edges. Make the buckle soft satin grey metal with one broad highlight in place of the bright chrome rim. Keep the house emblem and the wooden planks.
- p03: Boots: Draw the star and swirl decorations as smooth, broad raised yellow shapes in place of the dashed stitching. Make the red leather smooth with a soft satin sheen and no creases. Keep the spurs and the purple backdrop.
- p05: Campfire: Remove the log cabin and the kettle on its stone, and let soft pine shapes fill that space. Make the ring stones smooth grey with soft shading, with no speckles and no loose pebbles. Lighten the night sky slightly to a deep dusk blue; keep the fire and the logs.
