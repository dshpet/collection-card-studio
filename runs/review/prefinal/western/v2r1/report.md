## Art director

The generated cards are close to the real ones. The specialists picked the generated card on fewer than half the pairs, and both controls drew false flags. The hat holds up as is, and the cactus, beans and harmonica are each one or two small changes away. The tells that repeat across cards are small surface detail (stitching, rivets, bolts, speckle, mirror chrome), invented extras (the house sticker, spurs on the boots, pebbles, the pump's slab) and an all-over warm cast. Some heroes also sit small in a wide empty frame, but edit notes can't fix framing. The notes stay on finish, color, light and extras, with at most three changes each.

1. **Small surface detail and mirror metal. The generated cards add dashed stitching (spur straps, boot flames, wagon canvas), rivets, bolts and tacks (mailbox, harmonica caps, pump base, wagon), cast-iron speckle, and mirror chrome over the whole object (spurs, harmonica). Real cards show broad smooth satin surfaces with one or two soft highlights. This is the strongest tell and it shows on 6 of 10 generated cards.** Paint surfaces as broad, smooth painted or satin material with one soft broad highlight. Metal is satin grey, leather is plain and smooth, and canvas is plain cloth.
2. **Invented extras and scattered props: a house sticker on the mailbox, spurs on the boots, pebbles and grass tufts around the wagon and pump, and a stone slab under the pump. Real cards show the named object with a few quiet background shapes.** Show only the named object. Keep the ground and backdrop as a few smooth, quiet shapes.
3. **Warm cast across the whole card. The mailbox has a sunset sky with orange grass and road, the pump an orange sky and ground, the guitar sits orange on orange planks, and the beans are orange on orange wood with a warm-tinted rim. Real cards keep the backdrop cooler or neutral, so the hero separates by hue.** Use daylight skies in pale blue, natural mid-green grass, and grey or beige neutrals for stone and metal, so the backdrop contrasts with the hero's hue.
4. **Weak, frontal light. Shadow sides are shallow and contact shadows faint (boots, pump, beans), or the card has a spotlight vignette (guitar). Real cards use one upper-left key light, a clearly darker right side and a compact soft contact shadow.** Use one upper-left key light, a clearly darker right side and a soft compact contact shadow. Keep brightness even out to the corners.
5. **Plank floors and scenery drawn with heavy dark seams, knots and grain that compete with the hero (guitar, boots).** Draw floors as a few wide, smooth boards with faint seams.
6. **Small or elongated heroes float fully inside a wide empty field (spurs, harmonica, guitar, pump), while the real cards crop close. Notes cannot fix this because framing is out of scope for edits, so it is recorded here only.** Recorded only, no edit. Overruled: the shape_proportions and viewpoint_depth reports picked the real card on most pairs and flagged both controls. Their form-based calls cannot be judged across different objects, so they are dropped. The value_contrast call on control p09 is also dropped.

- p01: Mailbox: Remove the house picture from the door, leaving plain blue paint with one soft broad highlight and no scuffs or rivets. Change the sunset to a pale blue daytime sky, with natural mid-green grass and a muted beige dirt road. Keep the red flag, the knob and the post.
- p02: Baked Beans: Give the beans a soft satin sheen with fewer tiny highlights. Make the bowl rim a cool silver grey with one broad highlight. Make the soft shadow from the bowl onto the table to the lower right slightly darker. Keep the blue stripe, the spoon and the planks.
- p03: keep
- p05: Covered Wagon: Make the canvas plain smooth cloth with no stitch seams and no brass tacks, and the boards smooth with only faint grain. Remove the pebbles and grass tufts so the dirt road is a smooth gradient. Make the wagon's cast shadow slightly shorter and softer. Keep the red wheels and the sky.
- p06: Guitar: Make the floor a few wide, smooth boards in a slightly paler, even tone with faint seams and no knots. Light the card evenly with no darkened corners or spotlight. Give the guitar top one broad soft sheen and a soft contact shadow. Keep the red pickguard and the strings.
- p07: Spurs: Make the metal a satin grey with one soft highlight along each bar, in place of the mirror chrome. Make the straps plain smooth leather in a slightly deeper brown, with no stitching. Keep the rowels, the buckles and the blue rings.
- p08: Cowboy Boots: Remove the spurs. Paint the flame pattern as smooth red shapes with no dotted stitching. Make the right side of each boot clearly darker and add soft contact shadows under the soles. Keep the pull tabs, the floor and the striped wall.
- p10: Cactus Pot: Add soft, tone-on-tone purple rings behind the pot, evenly bright out to the corners. Keep the cactus, the flower, the pot and the shadow.
- p11: Harmonica: Make the cover a satin grey metal with one broad soft highlight along the top and a slightly darker front face, in place of the mirror streaks. Make the red end caps plain, with no rivets. Keep the holes and the cyan wave background.
- p12: Water Pump: Make the pump smooth green painted metal with one soft highlight down the left side, with no speckle, rust or bolts. Change the sky to pale blue and the ground to a soft beige-green with no scattered tufts or pebbles. Make the stone base a plain smooth grey. Keep the red handle and the spout.
