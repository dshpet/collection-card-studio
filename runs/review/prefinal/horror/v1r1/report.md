## Art director

Mixed. Three of ten generated cards pass as they are: the witch hat, the bat and the broom. They already match the real cards' bright, even backdrops, soft satin rendering and single soft light. The rest give themselves away through mood rather than form. The candelabra, crystal ball, gate and house use low-key practical lighting (glow pools, near-black tables, dark night fields). They also have photographic surface noise: dark wood grain, cobbles, chrome, hard glass highlights and per-shingle detail. The mirror and pumpkin sit on spotlit gradient backdrops where the real cards use even, patterned fields. All the notes stay within colour, value, light, finish and extras. The controls show that a white marble hero, a busy park and dense leaf texture all occur on real cards, so the specialists' lion and topiary calls were overruled.

1. **Moody practical lighting on dark grounds: candle and window glow pools on tables and lawns, near-black wood tables, a near-black night gate and a spotlit floor glow. The real cards light the hero with one soft daylight-style key, and even the real night card (motel) keeps the scene mid-value with many lit shapes. This is the strongest tell on the candelabra, crystal ball, gate and house.** Light the hero with one soft light from the upper left and give it a short, soft contact shadow. Keep the tables and floors a medium warm tone with a lit top plane. Let candle, window and inner glows light only the small area right next to them.
2. **Photographic surface noise that the real cards leave out: dark, high-contrast wood grain on tables, cobblestone fields, chrome reflections, hard window-shaped highlights, stock diagonal streaks on glass, per-shingle highlights and dense flowing grain on the tree trunk.** Paint surfaces as broad, smooth satin shapes with one soft highlight. Wood should be smooth with faint grain, paths should be a few large smooth slabs, glass should show one small soft round highlight, and metal should be satin.
3. **Plain backdrops drawn as a glowing spotlight gradient, with a dark vignette, a halo behind the hero and no floor line (mirror, pumpkin). The real plain-field cards keep one even mid-light value out to the corners, often with a quiet tone-on-tone pattern.** Make plain backdrops one even mid-light tone from the centre to the corners, with a quiet tone-on-tone stripe or ray pattern and a plain floor of the same colour.
4. **Invented story extras: crescent moons, the padlock and chain, scattered flame-light pools.** Show the object and its setting only. Leave the sky plain, with a few stars at most.
5. **Overruled: every specialist flagged the lion statue (p12), and several flagged the busy topiary (p04), but both are real controls. A white stone hero, marble veins, a full park setting and dense leaf texture all occur on real cards, so these are not tells. The edges specialist's picks on p03 (gloves) and p11 (balloons) and the composition pick on p12 (clock) were also wrong. All shape and proportion fixes are out of scope.** No style change. Do not make heroes more colourful or backgrounds simpler on the strength of these calls.

- p01: keep
- p02: keep
- p03: Dead Tree: Paint the trunk as smooth warm brown bark with only a few broad, soft grooves that follow the twist, in place of the dense grain lines. Make the lawn a slightly softer, less acid green, and make the cast shadow short, soft and attached at the roots. Keep the purple stripes and the curled branches.
- p05: Magic Mirror: Make the green backdrop one even mid green to the corners, with a quiet tone-on-tone stripe pattern and no dark corners or floor glow. Show the glass as a soft pale reflection with one gentle sheen, in place of the diagonal white streaks. Make the gold frame slightly satin, with one soft highlight band; keep the scrolls and the base.
- p06: Haunted House: Remove the moon. Paint the walls a warm cream-tan stone with a few faint block lines, and the roof slate-blue in broad soft rows with no per-tile highlights. Let the window light glow only just around each window, with no yellow pool on the grass; keep the tower, the chimney and the night sky.
- p07: Night Gate: Replace the cobblestones with a few large, smooth paving slabs in a light mauve. Make the pillars a warm sandstone with soft shading in place of the dark outlines and cracks, and lift the sky slightly to a medium dusk blue. Remove the moon, the chain and the padlock; keep the iron gate and the hedges.
- p08: Pumpkin: Make the purple backdrop one even mid violet to the corners, with a quiet tone-on-tone ray pattern and no glow behind the pumpkin. Add a plain purple floor plane under it with a crisp dark plum contact shadow. Shade the right side and the rib grooves slightly darker in burnt orange; keep the glowing face and the stem.
- p09: Candelabra: Make the striped wall a brighter mid purple and the table a lighter warm wood with smooth satin faint grain, with no orange light pools on it. Paint the candelabra a soft satin silver with cool blue-grey shadows, lit softly from the upper left. Keep the small candle flames, the candles and the stripes.
- p10: Crystal Ball: Make the table a lighter warm orange wood with smooth, faint grain and a soft short shadow, in place of the dark noisy wood. Give the ball one small soft round highlight in place of the hard window shape, and soften the smoke into gentle teal wisps. Keep the gold stand and the pink swirl.
- p11: keep
