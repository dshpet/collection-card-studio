## Art director

Close. The rendering, finish, saturation and backdrop types match the real cards. The train, star and hat could pass as they are, and the boot passes except for its backdrop. The main tell is warm on warm: orange wooden and leather western props on orange or yellow backdrops and floors, where the real cards put warm heroes on cool backdrops. The second tell is staging: icon wallpaper, scattered pebbles and flowers, a saloon door frame standing alone, a rail floating across the card, and heroes too small for the frame. The specialists' form, edge and viewpoint patterns are overruled. Nearly all of them picked the real map control as fake, and form can't be compared across different objects.

1. **Warm on warm. Western wood and leather props sit on orange, yellow or magenta backdrops and orange plank floors (boot, saddle rail, barrel, saloon doors, cactus). The whole card then reads as one orange mass. The real cards set a warm hero on a cool or contrasting backdrop: red scarf on cyan, chest on blue, popcorn cart on blue, boat on turquoise, jacket on green. This is the most visible tell across the set.** Put warm wooden or leather heroes on a soft sky-blue, teal or green backdrop. Use a paler, greyer beige for floors, so the hero is the only strong orange.
2. **Invented or unsupported staging: sun-and-wave icon wallpaper behind the barrel, scattered pebbles behind the cactus and the wagon, tiny flowers around the wagon, a saloon door frame standing alone on a floor, a saddle rail floating across a flat field. Real environments use a few large, quiet shapes, and every support is attached to something.** Use plain walls and smooth ground. Attach supports to something real, such as a fence on ground or a doorway in a wall.
3. **The hero is too small and leaves empty bands of background (lasso, wagon, cactus). The real cards fill the frame, and the object reaches near the edges.** Scale the hero up so it fills most of the card, with only a little backdrop around it.
4. **Some cards have flat, even light with faint contact shadows (saloon doors, saddle). Overruled as a general rule: the real map control (p04) is also lit flat and evenly, so only fix it where the card looks clearly washed out.** On those cards only, light from the upper left with a darker side and a soft contact shadow.
5. **Overruled. Shape proportions can't be judged across different objects, so the shape notes are ignored. Every specialist except staging_detail and composition_hierarchy picked the real map (p04 control) as fake: its crisp even contours, stacked repeated sheets, flat light and frontal view are all in the real cards. That overturns the 'uniform dark rim', 'identical repeated parts' and 'front-on view' patterns. The surface micro-texture claims are faint at card size: the boot grain and the hat felt are barely visible.** No change.

- p01: Make the lasso slightly larger and set it a little lower, so it fills the empty lower third of the card. Add a soft shadow on the purple under the coil and loop.
- p02: Change the orange rings to soft sky-blue rings with a pale centre glow. Keep the boot exactly as it is.
- p03: Rest the saddle on the top rail of a short wooden fence whose posts stand in soft green ground, with a simple soft sky behind. Paint the fence in weathered grey-tan wood. Light the saddle from the upper left so the right flap is slightly darker.
- p05: Replace the sun-and-wave icon wallpaper with a plain soft pale-blue wall. Make the floor planks a slightly paler grey-beige wood.
- p06: keep
- p07: Change the orange diamond backdrop to soft sky-blue diamonds. Make the sand a smooth dune with no scattered rocks. Draw the cactus slightly larger so its arms reach nearer the card edges.
- p08: Build the frame into a doorway in a plain teal wooden wall, so the posts belong to the wall. Paint the wood a slightly darker walnut brown. Light from the upper left, with darker post sides and a soft shadow on the floor under the doors.
- p09: keep
- p10: Bring the wagon closer so it fills most of the card. Make the road smooth dirt and the grass plain green, with no small flowers, pebbles or tufts. Put a deeper shadow under the wagon bed.
- p12: keep
