# Reference pilot, 2026-11-01

The first art analysis: nine specialist agents, one per art vector, each read four real cards in its own session, and a tenth merged their reports. `reports/raw/` holds the nine reports, `raw_synthesis.md` the merged answer, `SYNTHESIS.md` its readable form and `GENERATION_BRIEF.md` the style brief drawn from it.

Kept as a record. The evidence files it cites were not kept, and the brief was never tested: four cards proved too few, and the next day's analysis of all 160 cards replaced it ([analysis/SYNTHESIS.md](../../SYNTHESIS.md)). Its specialists became the reviewers in [analysis/agents/](../../agents/README.md).
