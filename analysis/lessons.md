# Lessons

Rules learned from the producer's ratings, in words the pipeline reads. The planner reads Planning. A producer can add, change or remove a line here to teach the tool, with no code change.

`scripts/experience.py propose` drafts new lessons from lost edits and rating comments under Proposed. Nothing under Proposed is used until a person moves it up.

## Planning

- Each card alone must make someone name the topic. Skip ordinary objects that fit only next to the others, such as an armchair or a rocking horse in a horror set. *(2026-11-03: Armchair and Rocking Horse read as the topic only beside the others)*
- The audience is wide and casual, of every age and faith. Nothing morbid, sad or divisive: no death or burial objects (coffins, graves, tombstones, skulls, bones), no religious symbols (crosses, holy books, altars), no illness, injury, drugs or politics. Haunted-house fun is fine: a pumpkin, a cauldron, a friendly ghost sheet. *(2026-11-04: the coffin and the tombstone with a cross)*
- In subject, give counts as the whole real object has them, not as one side shows them: a covered wagon has four wheels. *(2026-11-05: the two-wheeled wagon)*
- The accent in hero_colors colors a part the subject already names, never a new part: "a red band on the hat", not "a red gem". The image model draws every part the colors name, even when the subject says no. *(2026-11-05: every sheriff star got a red jewel; "no gem" in the subject left it in 3 of 3 draws, and moving the red onto the ring removed it in 3 of 3, experiments/plain_check.py)*
- Only real animals may appear as an object. A creature with a face, such as an alien, a monster or a robot, counts as a character. *(2026-11-05: Sci-Fi planned an Alien Pet and a friendly robot. 2026-11-06: a robot is half an object, kept out to be safe)*
- Every object has a logical, coherent structure that could stand and work in the real world. Each part named in the subject is attached to the main body or rests on the ground, and the subject says where it joins: "a ladder bolted to the hull, reaching the door at ground level." Don't plan loose or hanging accessories, such as railings, ropes, a ladder or a phone beside a booth. *(2026-11-05: Floating, unattached parts came up again and again: m04 "It just floas around the space" (twin/final), "Other objects just hang in space" (closest), "The railing is just hanging in the air unattached" (trial/control), "The railinig floats in the air" (trial/blockout), "floating the rail in space" (param). m20 "some of the ropes are floating through space". m15 "phone just floats on the outside" and "the phone is not placed correctly". sci-fi v1_10 "The ladder comes nowhere" and "Why the ladder is there and the entry is elevated? Makes no sense".)*
- Prefer objects with one solid, simple body. Skip objects that only look right if a complex structure is built correctly: folded paper, open frames and legs, supports and trusses, headbands with joints, suspension, or rails and tracks running into the distance. If the topic needs such an object, choose a closed, compact version of it. *(2026-11-06: kept for the test task, though it narrows variety. 2026-11-05: Structural failures repeated across rounds. Folded map m06: "Map is not folded correctly", "Incorrectly folded", "the map folding is not right", "not folded correctly". Waterslide m23: "can't function like that as a waterslide", "Structure is incorrect", "impossible in real world". Headphones m09: named in five comments, e.g. "Headphones are incorrect in all of those". Chair m16: "Chair structure is impossible", "the legs are not right". Pier m36: "AI slop without logic". Also western v1_10 "Perspective of the rails is broken" and sci-fi v1_07 "suspenion system... doesn't look right from engineering standpoint".)*
- In the subject, name the shape that makes the object recognizable, along with any symmetry or regular form it must keep: "a perfectly round clock face with numbers", "a symmetrical ball", "a lasso: a coiled rope ending in a wide open loop with a sliding knot." Don't leave the canonical form for the image model to guess. *(Tested 2026-11-05: "perfect circle" in the clock's subject left its roundness at 0.95 against 0.995 on the real card, experiments/shape_check.py. 2026-11-05: Clock m17: "Shape if the clock is not completely round", "Shape if off, not round", "All are not round", "Elogated shape of the clock". m05 "No numbers there. And the form is off". Ball m24: "Form of the ball is wrong and not symmetrical", "Proportions are off". Lasso western v1_03: "The ring is somewhat malformed and doesn't look like a traditional lasso" and "The shape of the rope in the lasso is not 'lasso-y'". Sci-fi v1_02 "Somewhat not symmetrical and broken".)*
- Put the object on the surface or in the setting where it really stands, and say so in the subject, e.g. a potted plant on grass, not on a road. Don't plan placements or surroundings that would be illogical in the real world. *(2026-11-05: m34: "Strange placement", "strange incoherent placement and not logical overall picture", "The plant is being placed on the road, which is very strange. In the original it is placed on the grass". m36: "Stupid object in physical world, not correctly situated". western v1_08: "the placement is not ideal maybe".)*
- build_model is true for a coiled rope with a working loop, such as a lasso, and for a badge or emblem with a precise outline, though they look simple. *(2026-11-05, from the blockout redraw ratings: Lasso 2 slightly off to 3 works, Star Badge 2 to 4, Telescope 3 to 5, Saddle 2 to 4. Two cards for this rule, so it is thin evidence.)*
- A card may show fire, described in the subject as on the real Campfire Cook card: "a bright fire with a yellow-white glowing core, orange flame tips curling upward, glowing orange ends on the logs, and warm orange light cast on what stands near it." Never leave it as a small flame in hero_colors. *(2026-11-06, experiments/fire_check.py: the cauldron's planned "small orange flame" drew a tiny flame in 3 of 3; this wording, with "big" in it, drew a bright fire in 3 of 3, bright fire share 0.010 to 0.076 against 0.034 on the real card, so "big" is dropped here. The real fire cards as references added nothing and pulled the Lantern's sunburst into the backdrop. Earlier: cauldron horror v1_04 "Fire is too small and the pot is kinda broketn" (grade 1), "Fire is laughable" (grade 1); sci-fi meteor v1_04 "But the fire - they don't have it anywhere". The producer: glowing effects could be good. Accepted 2026-11-06.)*
- In scenes with characters, plan no action or difference in treatment that could read as harm, violence or neglect, such as kicking, hitting or one character protected while another is exposed. Gear must make sense for every character and fit its body: if the setting needs a helmet, every character gets one that fits its head, crest and ears. Otherwise, choose a setting that needs no helmet. *(2026-11-06: card/sci-fi-batch/v1_10: "The guy is in helmet, but the pig isn't. Which could be some sort of animal cruelty"; card/horror-batch/v1_10: "the pumpkin being kicked... May look like violence"; cast/paste/scifi_rooster_paste_2: "Should not use helmet, it's broken with his peackock". A calm scene scored best: western-batch v1_10, Campfire Pals sitting by the fire, grade 5, "Very good". Accepted 2026-11-06.)*
- Each object must be named from its picture alone, without the title, by a player from any country. Skip invented devices, such as a teleport pod, and foods or customs known in only one country, such as candy corn. *(2026-11-06: horror-batch v1_06 Candy Corn, grade 2: "Strange object, unrecognizable. The art style if fine though"; sci-fi-batch v1_07 Teleport Pod, grade 2: "The object itself looks strange - I wouldn't know what it is without the title". The first planning rule already asks for instantly recognizable objects, and the planner still chose both. Accepted 2026-11-06.)*
- A part made of a natural material keeps that material's color: a cork stopper is light brown, a wooden handle wood brown, a rope tan. Put the accent in hero_colors on a part that could really be any color. *(2026-11-06: horror-batch v1_07 Potion, grade 4: "the cork should be of cork colour"; the plan put the accent on the cork as "a small green label-free cork". One card. Accepted 2026-11-06.)*
