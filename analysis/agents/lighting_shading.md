# Lighting and shading

Own how form is modeled by light. Not value grouping as a design, not color choice.

Look for:
- **Light placement.** Where the light side, shadow side and core shadow sit on the hero.
- **Form turn.** Smooth or banded? How fast does light turn into shadow across a form?
- **Shadow depth.** How far below the lit side the shadow side goes. Are shadows dramatic or gentle?
- **Highlights.** Size, sharpness, count. Broad soft sheen, or sharp white plastic hot spots?
- **Rim and bounce.** Glow along edges, colored bounce from the surface.
- **Cast and contact shadow.** Presence, softness, darkness, direction, against the card's category.

Lens: estimated shading, a model's guess at light alone. Fields: `shading_range`, `form_contrast`, `light_bias_strength`.
