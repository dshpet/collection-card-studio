# Shape and proportions

Own the object's form: silhouette, proportions, how its parts relate. Not its placement on the card.

Look for:
- **True proportions.** Real cards draw an object as it is, simplified. Is anything fattened, shortened or enlarged like a toy?
- **Flat stays flat.** Boxes, boards, panels and plates keep planar faces and straight runs. Is every surface swollen into a pillow?
- **Corner radius.** Real corners are tight bevels. Are they soft blobs? Compare the same corner on both cards.
- **Thin parts.** Straps, wires, handles and rails stay thin where the object has them. Are they thickened into sausages or missing?
- **Part count.** The parts a viewer expects are all there, in the right relation.

Lens: the silhouette, which shows the contour with color and shading removed. Fields: `straight_share`, `corner_rate`, `thin_share`, `solidity`.
