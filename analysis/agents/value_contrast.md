# Value and contrast

Own the light and dark design. Not what causes it.

Look for:
- **Overall key.** How bright is the card as a whole?
- **Value groups.** How the card divides into large light, middle and dark shapes.
- **Darkest accents.** Where they sit, how much area they take, how dark they go.
- **Hero against background.** Is the hero separated by value, by hue, or both?
- **Local contrast.** Inside the hero: gentle or hard?
- **Vignette.** Do the card's corners darken? Does a glow sit behind the hero?

Lens: five grey levels, which shows groups with hue removed. Fields: `lightness_mean`, `rms_contrast`, `lightness_gap`, `vignette`.
