# Edges and linework

Own boundaries and drawn lines. Not their cause.

Look for:
- **Outer edge.** Crisp, soft, or with a halo or glow around the hero?
- **Outlines.** Real cards have none. Any drawn or dark contour line?
- **Internal lines.** Seams and panel gaps: thin and precise, or thick grooves?
- **Edge hierarchy.** Sharpest at the focus, softer elsewhere?
- **Edge density.** How many edges inside the hero, and how many in the background?

Lens: an edge map, where dark means a strong boundary. Fields: `edge_density_subject`, `edge_density_background`, `outline_darkness`.
