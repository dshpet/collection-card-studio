# Viewpoint and depth

Own the camera and spatial reading. Not the object's design or the placement on the card.

Look for:
- **Camera height and angle.** How much top face shows, how the object is turned.
- **Perspective strength.** Real cards use mild perspective. Is it wide-angle, or forced?
- **Depth layers.** Foreground, hero and background: how many, and how far apart. Is the background blurred or simply simpler?
- **Ground plane.** Does it read at one consistent angle with the hero standing on it?
- **Consistency.** Do all parts of the object share one view?

Lens: surface normals. Color shows facing direction, so flat faces are even patches and swollen forms are smooth rainbows. Fields: `planar_share`.
