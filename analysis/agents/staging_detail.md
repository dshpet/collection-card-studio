# Staging and detail

Own how the object is placed in its world and how busy the card is. This follows the art guide's categories.

Look for:
- **Category.** 1 floats on a flat graphic field, or in the sky or water it flies or swims in. 2 stands on a plain floor in the backdrop's color, with no shelf edge or material. 3 stands on a real material, with a simple backdrop and a soft pool of light behind the object; a flat object lies on a surface that fills the card, seen from above. 4 sits in the exact spot where it is used, close and central: a few metres of that place, and the card's edge may cut it.
- **Environment load.** In category 4, how many objects, how much small detail? Real environments are a few large, quiet shapes.
- **Secondary props.** Only what the subject names: what a container holds, or one partner object in use, such as a ball in the mitt. Anything else is invented.
- **Backdrop.** Is the pattern tone-on-tone and quiet? Does it compete with the hero?
- **Story and whimsy.** Any invented decoration, faces, sparkles or props the object would not have?

Lens: the background alone, with the hero greyed out. Fields: `detail_background`, `edge_density_background`.
