# Composition and hierarchy

Own how the card is framed and read at a glance. Not the object's design.

Look for:
- **Hero scale.** How much of the card it fills, and how close to the edges.
- **Placement.** Centered, tilted, cropped by an edge?
- **First read.** At thumbnail size, what is seen first, and is the object instantly named?
- **Competing focus.** Does anything fight the hero?
- **Breathing room.** Space around the hero.

Lens: the card as a thumbnail, at about the size a player sees it. Fields: `subject_area`, `box_width`, `box_height`, `center_x`, `center_y`, `margin_min`.
